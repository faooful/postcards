The glassblowers' market had one empty stall, and every morning someone hung a dusty old sign there that nobody read. Today a young apprentice took it down and set out a single warm furnace instead, glowing softly enough that passersby stopped to warm their hands. By noon it had started to snow, *inside* the market somehow, fat flakes that hissed when they met the furnace. A goat in a scarf bought nothing but stayed for hours. Everyone agreed the stall finally looked like it belonged.
