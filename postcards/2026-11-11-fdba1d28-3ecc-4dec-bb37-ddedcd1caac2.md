Up in Fennimore Gap, every family had carved its own bench for the evening concerts, and no two were the same height. The goats noticed first. Over one foggy week they nudged each bench into a single long row, nibbling the tall legs shorter and propping the short ones on flat stones. On Sunday the whole village sat down at once, knees level, and the brass band found it could finally see every face. Nobody admitted to liking it. Everyone stayed past dark.
