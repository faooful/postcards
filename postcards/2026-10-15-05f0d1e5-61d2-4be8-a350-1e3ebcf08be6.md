The otters of Reedmarsh held their regatta this week, and someone finally thought to chalk lanes on the water. Of course the chalk drifted, but the otters liked the idea so much that each one guarded its own stripe. Nobody bumped noses. A heron called out the order of crossing, slowly and with great ceremony, and the smallest otter paddled home first, grinning, carrying a pebble she swore was a trophy. I am keeping one too.
