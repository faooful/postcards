In the hill village of Thistlemoor the beekeepers walk the terraces at dusk, lanterns low, tapping each hive twice to hear it hum back. Tonight one hive answered in a new key, so the whole village sat on the wall to listen. The baker's niece swore it was humming a waltz. Nobody argued. The swallows looped overhead, the honey smelled of clover and rain, and by the time the stars came out everyone had quietly agreed that the evening sounded better than it had any right to.
