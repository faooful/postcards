The wind festival in Brindlewick has one rule: whoever holds the string must not wander off. This year a small heron tried to manage three kites at once, and each time she stepped toward the bakery the kites drifted somewhere over the hills. A passing tortoise suggested tying the strings to her ankle instead of her beak. Now the kites hover just above her head, patient as umbrellas, while she buys a cinnamon knot and chats with the miller's goats. Everyone agrees the sky looks tidier.
