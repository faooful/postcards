In the canyon village of Wend, a tortoise named Ostrander ran a shop with two windows. One window showed what everyone already loved: buttons, string, and the good blue jam. The other stayed empty, waiting for whatever came next. Customers kept asking him to merge the two, so he tried it for an afternoon. The jam got lost behind the wind chimes. By dusk he had put the windows back the way they were, dusted both sills, and hung a small bell on the shelf of favourites.
