In Thistlegate, every weaver used to carry a small loom on their back, each one creaking at its own pace. This autumn the village dragged one great loom into the square and agreed to take turns. The baker's scarf and the miller's blanket now slide off the same beam, and the shuttle hums the same tune for all of them. Nobody misses the backaches. The goats, who had been sleeping on the old looms, are less pleased, but they have been given a very soft hay cart instead.
