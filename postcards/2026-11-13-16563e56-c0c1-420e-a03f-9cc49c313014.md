In Wrenmoor the cellar flooded with plums nobody remembered pickling, and the town sent its most patient auditor, a heron named Odile, to sort the jars. She held each one up to the single window, read the sediment like tea leaves, and chalked a verdict on the lid: *keep*, *taste first*, or *ask the river*. By dusk the shelves stood in tidy rows, and one jar, stubbornly unlabeled, turned out to be the best of the lot.
