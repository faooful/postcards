The Bellwether Goat Choir set out at noon to sing at a wedding on top of Crumbling Knoll. Halfway up, a gust blew their sheet music into a ravine, so they sat on the road and hummed until the wind got bored. Then mud swallowed the third switchback, and they waited again, chewing thoughtfully. By dusk they reached the summit, a little damp, and sang the first verse so steadily that the bride's grandmother forgot to complain about the cake.
