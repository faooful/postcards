The goats of Brindle Ridge run a gallery on a cliff, and every spring the paintings drift sideways in the wind. This year the eldest goat had an idea: instead of more nails, frames. Each painting got a frame of its own, the tall ones moved to the top row, and the little sketch of a sleeping marmot stopped hanging off the edge. Nothing new went on the walls. Visitors still swore the place felt twice as tidy, and the marmot looked very pleased.
