In the river town of Alder Bend, the timetable board had always lived behind glass, in a frame so heavy that nobody read past the first sailing. One morning the signwriter simply took the frame away. The columns stayed. The grey band at the top stayed. But now the departures ran straight into the air of the waiting room, and people leaned in. A heron paused on the rail, *considered the 10:40*, and nodded. Nothing about the boats had changed. Only the room felt larger.
