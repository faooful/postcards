The cable tram to Pellwick only ran when someone rang the little brass bell at the bottom station, and for years the bell sat behind a locked hatch nobody could find. This week a goat named Orrin nosed the hatch open, rang it twice, and the whole car lurched upward through the mist. Passengers clapped. The conductor, still in slippers, handed out warm chestnuts and pretended the bell had always been within reach.
