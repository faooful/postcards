On the floe at Whistlebay, the penguin choir stands in rows by age, oldest at the front. This winter a chick hatched mid-rehearsal and, rather than shuffle to the back where nobody could hear her, the elders simply budged over and let her stand first. Her voice was thin and wobbly and *entirely* delightful. The walruses in the audience clapped their flippers so hard the ice hummed. By spring, every new singer starts at the front, and the old ones say the songs feel younger for it.
