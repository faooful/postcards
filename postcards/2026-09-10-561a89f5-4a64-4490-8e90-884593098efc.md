Dear reader — the ferrymen of Lantern Hollow used to wave at arriving boats only once, at dawn, then declare the harbor settled for good. Skiffs that arrived at noon bobbed unnoticed for hours. We taught them a new habit: look again whenever the bell settles, and hum a slow tune while the kettle still whistles. Yesterday they caught a red-sailed dinghy the instant it rounded the pier, and waved back before it could feel invisible. The dinghy, they say, circled twice for joy.
