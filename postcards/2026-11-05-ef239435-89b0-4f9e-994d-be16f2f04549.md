Every autumn the marsh frogs of Saltmarsh Ridge haul an old sail across four driftwood posts and declare it Trampoline Day. Nobody keeps score. A young frog named Pell, shy all summer, finally climbs up, crouches, and goes *up* three times, each hop a little more certain than the last. On the third landing the whole reed bank hums its approval. Pell sits on the warm canvas afterward, dizzy and delighted, watching the dragonflies practice the same trick in the low gold light.
