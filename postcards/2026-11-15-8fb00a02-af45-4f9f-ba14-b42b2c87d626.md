Every hard frost, the hedgehogs of Nettlebottom climb the cottage sills with tiny ledgers and name the ice-ferns before the sun can edit them. Whorl, feather, bracken-star — each crystal gets a line and a small checkmark in pencil. Yesterday's frost was the finest of the season, and the whole committee stayed past dawn to finish the page, quills trembling with cold and pride. By spring they will hold a complete atlas of winter, drawn entirely from windows nobody wiped too soon.
