The snails of Brindle Marsh vote by climbing reeds, and this year the reeds were crowded with tiny banners nobody could read. So the eldest snail, a patient creature named Wendle, proposed a new rule: only climb if you have something to say. By dusk the marsh looked wonderfully calm. A few bright shells glowed at the tips of the tallest reeds, all perched at exactly the same height, and the frogs agreed it was the clearest election in living memory.
