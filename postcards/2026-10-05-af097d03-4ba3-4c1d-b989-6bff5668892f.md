At Little Brindle Crossing the ferry door only opens for passengers who linger. Hurry past and it stays politely shut; pause a breath on the gangplank and two otters in waistcoats swing it wide. Once you are aboard they wave everyone else through without a second's wait, until the last traveller steps ashore and they settle back to their knitting. Today a fog rolled in smelling of cinnamon, and nobody minded the slow start. *The best doors,* the captain said, *know when you mean it.*
