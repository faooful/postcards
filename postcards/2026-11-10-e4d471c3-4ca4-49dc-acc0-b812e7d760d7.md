In the hill town of Quillmarsh, the kites had always waved politely and gone nowhere. Children tugged and tugged, and the kites only nodded back, like shy cousins at a wedding. This autumn a tinker with a cheerful limp tied one small knot differently. Now a single tap on the spool and the kite carries you, gently, right over the hedge to wherever it was pointing. The goats are scandalized. The children are delighted, and the bakery has never had so many sudden visitors arriving from the sky.
