Every winter the fairground at Ninepin gets one blue week, and that is when Mira opens the carousel. The painted beasts step off their poles at midnight — a thylacine, a sea mink, a great auk with copper feathers — and amble between the ticket booths, leaving hoofprints that smell faintly of peppermint. Children arrive in mittens to ride bareback until the sky pales. Mira just sweeps the platform and keeps the cocoa warm. The animals never say where they spend the other fifty-one weeks, and she has decided that is their business.
