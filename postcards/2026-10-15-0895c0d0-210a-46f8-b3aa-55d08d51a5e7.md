Every autumn the greenhouse drifts loose from its moorings and floats over the village, and every autumn the beavers send an inspector aboard. This year she found the ferns thriving, the misting pipes humming, and one stubborn trellis that leaned across the potting bench so the gardener could not reach her seedlings. She tied a red ribbon to it, wrote a careful note, and climbed down a rope of ivy. *Almost perfect,* she told the mayor, *and almost is a lovely place to start.*
