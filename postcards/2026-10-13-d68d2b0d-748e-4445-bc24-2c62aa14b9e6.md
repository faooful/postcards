At dusk the beetles of the salt flats lit their tiny lanterns and began, as always, to sweep. Nobody remembered who had asked them to. This evening a gust scattered the crystals into loops and spirals, and the youngest beetle, Pim, decided the spirals were too pretty to tidy. The elders grumbled, then wandered off to admire them too. By midnight the whole flat glittered like a map of somewhere kind, and everyone slept *well*, swept or not.
