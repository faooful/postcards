The ferry at Pellwick is too narrow for the village band, so every Thursday they fold themselves in. The tuba sits sideways and the drummer takes the coil of rope for a stool. The accordion player plays only the middle of each song. Nobody falls overboard. Gulls drift over to count the hats and never get the same number twice. By the far landing the music has squeezed into one bright chord. It is somehow wider than the river.
