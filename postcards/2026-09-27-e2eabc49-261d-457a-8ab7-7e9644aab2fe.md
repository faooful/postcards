At dusk, a clockmaker found a blue comet resting in the drawer where he kept spare minutes. It was no larger than a button, but it hummed like a sky full of bees.

He carried it to the town square, where the old sundial had forgotten which way to point. The comet rose, tugged the shadow into place, and scattered a little blue light across every waiting face.

By morning, nobody agreed on the exact time. Everyone agreed it had been worth staying up for.
