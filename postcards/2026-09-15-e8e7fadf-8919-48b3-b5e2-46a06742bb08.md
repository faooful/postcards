The night ferry radio crackled: "Signal's not landing." Mira had rebuilt the relay tower three times, certain each version was flawless, before she noticed the real trouble — the tower kept declaring victory the instant it sent a message, never once checking if anyone downriver had actually caught it. She rewired it to wait for an echo back. That evening, for the first time in weeks, the far shore answered right on cue, and the whole valley exhaled in unison.
