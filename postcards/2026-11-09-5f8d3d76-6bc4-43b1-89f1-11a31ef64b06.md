When the first hail cloud bruised the sky above Vellum Valley, the paper wasps of the old haymow decided the evening needed a dance. They stitched a spiral through the rafters, each turn a little braver than the last, until the whole barn hummed like a struck wire. A mouse kept time with a thimble. Everybody was terrible. Everybody kept going, and the hail passed over, embarrassed, and went to bother some other valley.
