In Foldham Hollow the aurora wandered lower than usual and settled, for one evening, right above the laundry lines. The night baker came out for her last tray of rye and found her pillowcases glowing a polite green, so she left the oven door ajar and joined the neighbors in the yard. Someone fetched a fiddle. The sheets swayed in time, the pins held a steady rhythm, and by midnight every shirt in the hollow was faintly luminous and nobody minded the extra ironing that follows a dance.
