Down in the Map Room of Quillfen, the night shift found the atlas of side corridors missing one small mark — the angle-bracket stamp that told travelers which corridors carried stories. An apprentice mixed a fresh pot of ink, pressed the old brass stamp back into its cradle, and nudged it until it sat exactly level with the corridor seals beside it. By morning every corridor legend began where the seals began, and the travelers who skimmed the margins noticed the row finally breathing together.
