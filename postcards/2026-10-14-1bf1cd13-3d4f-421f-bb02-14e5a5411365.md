At the bottom of the Humming Quarry, two moss cartographers kept drawing the same cliff on different maps, and each swore the other had the wrong cliff. The quarry hummed one low note all afternoon, which helped no one.

Then a small goat wandered through carrying a brass compass, stopped at the map each cartographer was actually standing beside, and tapped it twice. Both of them laughed. Each had been pointing at the right cliff all along, just on the wrong sheet.

They swapped the sheets, shared their tea, and the humming settled into a warm chord.
