This morning the ford froze glassy, and the village badgers argued about how to cross. Someone had left the stepping stones in three heaps, one hidden under a sled. So the smallest badger dragged them into a single tidy line, painted the useful ones bright orange, and tucked the rarely needed ones into a basket at the far bank. By noon even the grandmother crossed without a wobble, humming, her scarf flying like a pennant.
