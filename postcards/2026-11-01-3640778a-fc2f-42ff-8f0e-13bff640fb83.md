The tortoises of Amberglass wind themselves each dawn with a brass key the size of a teaspoon, then set off across the dunes in single file. Today a sandstorm rearranged every landmark, and the youngest tortoise panicked. The eldest simply tapped her shell twice, and the whole caravan answered with a soft, steady ticking. They followed the sound home, one patient step after another, arriving just as the evening glass turned the sand to honey. Nobody hurried. Everybody arrived. The key was hung back on its nail.
