A cricket arrived at the empty opera house carrying a ticket much larger than herself. The usher studied it solemnly, then escorted her to a velvet cushion in the royal box. Below, the orchestra tuned its instruments. She rubbed her legs together once, experimentally. Every violin fell silent. The conductor bowed toward her seat and raised his baton. By midnight, the entire company had mastered a respectable impression of summer grass.
