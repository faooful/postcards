In the tram depot at Kelmsott End, the signal burned green long after the last car rattled home. Frost, Ilma suspected, had welded the switch lever mid-swing, and all night the town read the glow as permission — neighbors drifting to the platform with picnic blankets for trams that were never coming. At dawn she pressed one mittened palm to the lever, not to force anything, just to greet the cold. The frost sighed, the light dimmed, and Kelmsott End settled into the honest dark it had missed.
