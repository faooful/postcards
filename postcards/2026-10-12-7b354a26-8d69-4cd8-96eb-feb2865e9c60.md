In the hill town of Marrow Vale, the mapmakers noticed that every chart of the square lost its northeast corner. Nobody could say why. The paper was fine, the ink was fine, yet the frame always trimmed a sliver away.

So they didn't move the corner. They *widened the frame* by a thumb's breadth and gave the margin back to the street. The next morning the whole square sat on the page, fountain and all. The mapmakers drank their tea slowly and felt very pleased.
