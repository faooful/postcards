At dawn the quarry-town of Pellwick woke to find its stairways rearranged again. A cartographer named Odalie decided to stop redrawing every map and instead pencil one faint, temporary stairway that the wind could erase. Travelers who only wished to peek at a ledge used it, and it simply faded behind them. Those who stayed, tapping the stone twice, found their path inked permanently. By evening, the town's maps were calm and uncluttered, and Odalie slept soundly, a single pale line still fluttering at the margin of her page.
