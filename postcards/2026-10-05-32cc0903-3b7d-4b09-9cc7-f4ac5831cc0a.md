Beneath the deserted bowling alley, a brass badger has opened a dance school. His pupils are dust bunnies, so the footwork is mostly theoretical. Tonight they attempted a polka and accidentally swept the entire floor.

Nobody minded. The badger awarded everyone a ribbon made of ribbon, which was considered refreshingly practical. Then he rolled a marble into the middle of the room and announced the moon had arrived for its private lesson.
