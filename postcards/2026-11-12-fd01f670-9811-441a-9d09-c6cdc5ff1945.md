In Vesna's workshop the furnace never quite sleeps; it keeps a low orange hum all night, like a cat that has decided to stay. This evening a hairline crack opened along a bowl's shoulder, and the whole room leaned in while she rolled the pipe back into the flame. Nothing was wasted — the glass simply asked for one more breath. By morning the bowl held light the way a pond holds a passing cloud: briefly, completely, as if it had always meant to.
