The village forecast promised a light scattering of apricots, so everyone brought a spoon. By noon, the orchard had politely declined to participate. Instead, a small cloud arrived carrying a picnic blanket and asking directions to the nearest hill.

We went together. Nothing remarkable fell from the sky, unless you count the afternoon slowly settling around us. The cloud shared its sandwiches. They were mostly air, but the mustard was excellent.
