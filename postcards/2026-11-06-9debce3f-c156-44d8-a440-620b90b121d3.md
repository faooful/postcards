In Cindervale the chimney sweeps keep a chalkboard at the foot of the last flue, and the custom is simple: refresh the line whenever the sky changes, not only when the foreman comes down from the roof. Today Marta swept four chimneys and chalked each one as she finished, so by dusk the board read plainly: two capped, one rebricked, one waiting on rain. The town read it at supper instead of climbing up to ask. She wiped the smudges, kept her lines short, and caught the last tram down feeling thoroughly current.
