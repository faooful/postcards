In the flooded quarry at Vell, the glassworms come out only when it rains sideways. Tonight a girl named Oriel held two jars up to the drizzle, one from last spring and one from this morning, and swore the light inside them was the same shade exactly. The old stonecutters laughed and squinted and held them up too. Nobody could find a single difference. They agreed to keep both jars on the windowsill, side by side, and went home dry-footed and oddly pleased.
