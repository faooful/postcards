Every evening in Thistlewick the clocktower plays itself, but this autumn the bells forgot their order. So the town bees volunteered. Three swarms took a bell each, and a very serious bumblebee in a paper hat stood on the weathervane waving a dandelion stem. The first try sounded like a kettle arguing with a goose. The second was nearly a waltz. By the third, the baker stopped mid-loaf and the ducks lined up on the canal to listen. Nobody can hum it now without smiling.
