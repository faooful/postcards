The snail caravan set out at dawn, each shell painted a different color so the scouts could tell them apart from the ridge. By noon the dunes had turned the color of old pennies and hummed faintly underfoot. The youngest snail kept stopping to sketch the tracks other travelers had left: three-toed, spiral, one that looked like someone dragging a harp. Nobody knew who made them. *Good*, she wrote in the margin. *Then we get to guess which way is new.*
