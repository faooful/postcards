The open-air picture show came to Dunwhistle on a Tuesday, and a hedgehog named Pell volunteered to arrange the seats. Every guest had brought a chair far too wide: a sofa, a throne, one hammock strung between two goats. Pell nudged and tucked and folded until the whole crowd fit inside the chalk line, nobody spilling into the dunes. When the film began, the projector hummed, the goats sighed, and the salt glowed softly, like everyone had been given exactly enough room.
