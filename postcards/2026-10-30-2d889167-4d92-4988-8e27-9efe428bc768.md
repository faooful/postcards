In the river town of Pebblewick, the otters who run the boarding house had always aired one room at a time, waiting politely for each to finish breathing before moving on. This autumn the eldest otter declared that rooms could share the breeze. So every shutter swung wide together, and the hallway filled with drifting maple leaves, the smell of rain, and a very confused moth. Nobody's room got the whole wind anymore, but everybody got enough. The guests said it was the coziest week on record.
