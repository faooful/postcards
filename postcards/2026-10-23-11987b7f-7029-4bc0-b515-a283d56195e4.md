On the canals of Vessaline, the ferries used to drift wherever the current felt like taking them. This week a committee of otters took charge. Each boat now wears a little painted number on its prow, and a single whistle sends any one of them gliding to the steps. Two whistles bring back the ferry that left too soon. The gondoliers grumbled for an afternoon, then started whistling along. By evening, even the herons were waiting their turn at the landing.
