Every evening in Brindlemoss Square, the old accordion player folds and unfolds the market awnings with his music. Lately they had stuck open, flapping like startled geese, and the pigeons kept landing on the wrong stalls. Tonight he tried a slower waltz. One awning sighed shut, then another, and the spice seller finally found her own roof again. A small boy asked which song makes the stars come out. The player winked and said that one was still in rehearsal.
