At Marrowgate Station two choirs boarded the same night train by mistake, each sure the dining car was theirs. The conductor, a patient badger in a velvet cap, gave every singer a seat ticket stamped with their own choir's color and let them keep the same hymns. By the third tunnel they had stopped trading elbows and started trading harmonies. *Nobody lost their place*, the badger wrote in the logbook, and then, after some thought, underlined it twice.
