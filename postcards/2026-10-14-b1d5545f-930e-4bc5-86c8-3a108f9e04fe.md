The tower keeper on duty insisted on reading every flag twice before logging it settled. Her predecessor had waved the same banner through once, and the wind had changed its meaning by morning. So she climbed back up with a lantern, checked the rope held, watched the cloth catch three times running in different gusts, and only then wrote the entry calm and certain. Down at the dock, nobody noticed the extra trip up the stairs — only that the signal, when it mattered, said exactly what it meant.
