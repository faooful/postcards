In Larkspur Gulch the bees forgot which hive was home, so the beekeepers held a dance. Each hive got a painted ribbon, and every keeper carried one, twirling it whenever a swarm drifted past. *Follow the blue!* someone sang, and the bees did, buzzing politely into their own doorways. By dusk the meadow was humming in tidy rows. Nobody was lost, though a sleepy drone did a lap of the whole valley first, just to check every ribbon was where it belonged.
