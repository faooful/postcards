Up in Quillstone Valley the two old glaciers used to sprawl wherever they liked, one across the meadow, one half over the goat path. This autumn they agreed, after a long and creaky meeting, to share a single ledge along the eastern wall. The goats were delighted. The marmots held a small parade with acorn drums. When the afternoon fog rolled in, both glaciers slid aside in perfect unison, as if they had rehearsed it all summer, and everyone below cheered.
