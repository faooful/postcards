The goat boarded at midnight with a ticket she had chewed only slightly. The conductor, a tall heron-necked woman with a brass whistle, pretended not to notice. Somewhere past the salt cliffs the train leaned into a long curve, and every sleeping passenger tipped gently the same way, like wheat in a breeze. The goat pressed her nose to the window. Below, the valley towns blinked out one by one, unhurried, as if someone were tucking them in.
