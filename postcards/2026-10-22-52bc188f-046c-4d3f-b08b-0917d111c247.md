At Hollin Fair the hot-air balloonists used to drift low over the bandstand, bumping hats and startling the tuba. This year someone painted a neat lane along the meadow's eastern edge, and the balloons settled there in a tidy row, close enough to wave at but never in the way. When the wind turned crowded they rose and hovered politely above the crowd instead. The tuba player says it's the calmest he's felt in years. Even the goats approve.
