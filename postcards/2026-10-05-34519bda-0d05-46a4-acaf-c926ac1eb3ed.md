In the hill town of Quillmere, an accordion player wanders the terraces at dusk, and the sunflowers swivel to follow him like a crowd at a tennis match. Nobody planted them for this. When he stops to tie a shoe, they all lean in at once, deeply curious. A goat tried to join the audience and was politely ignored. Tonight he played a waltz from the very top step, and every bloom in the valley tipped its face upward, *delighted*, as though the evening had turned toward them.
