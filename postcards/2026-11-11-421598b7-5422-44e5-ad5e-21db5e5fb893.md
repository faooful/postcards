In the hill village of Tarrowmere, travelers kept arriving at the right cottage with no idea which valley they were in. So the night owls took up a new habit. Before guiding anyone up the lane, an owl would first fly to the ridge, check which hamlet the cottage belonged to, and only then swoop down with a small folded map. Now every guest steps inside already knowing the river, the bakery, and the way home. The owls seem very pleased with themselves.
