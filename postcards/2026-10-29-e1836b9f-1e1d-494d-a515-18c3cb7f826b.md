Halfway up Mount Pellucid, the relay had stopped. Three snails sat on three ledges, each holding a pebble, each waiting for the one below to move. Nobody remembered who was supposed to go first. Then a cricket wandered by, nudged the lowest snail, and the pebble began to climb again: ledge to ledge, slow and certain, glinting in the afternoon. By dusk the summit flag was fluttering, and the snails agreed, very politely, that next time they would simply ask.
