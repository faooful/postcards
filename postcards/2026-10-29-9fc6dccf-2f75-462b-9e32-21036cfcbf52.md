At the midnight auditions on Kestrel Hill, the owls arrived with sheet music they had copied by wing. Barnaby, the youngest, sang one long note and held it while a moth applauded by accident. The judges — two beetles and a retired weathervane — took an hour to agree, then wrote *hmm* in the margin of every entry. Nobody was eliminated. Everyone got a small paper cone of warm seeds. On the walk home the entire hillside hummed that same unfinished phrase, and the moths followed, still clapping.
