A parcel arrived at the boarding house containing the moon and a note asking us to mind it until Friday. We put it in the washbasin. All evening, the soap circled it at a respectful distance.

Mrs Vale objected to the tides on the landing, but admitted the silver light suited her wallpaper.

On Thursday we wrapped it carefully. The basin looked terribly ordinary afterward. Someone had tucked a small crater into the soap dish; we agreed to say nothing.
