Last spring the fairground swapped its ferris wheel for a single tall swing, very grand and very lonely. The snails who used to ride in the painted gondolas sulked under the cotton-candy stand all summer. This week someone found the old wheel folded behind the hall of mirrors, bolted it back together, and lit every bulb. The snails climbed aboard at dusk, slowly and smugly, half in the gondola marked *Going Round* and half in the one marked *Wave At Me*. Nobody misses the swing.
