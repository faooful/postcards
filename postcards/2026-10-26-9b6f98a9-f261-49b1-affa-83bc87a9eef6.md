The balloonist of Marrowfen never lands. She drifts low over the reed villages, close enough to wave, high enough that no one has to move their washing lines for her. Children run beneath her shadow shouting the names of clouds. When a farmer calls up a question, she drops a ribbon with the answer tied to the end, then floats on without stopping. Today the wind is soft and kind. *Everything below keeps its room*, she writes in her logbook, *and I keep the sky.*
