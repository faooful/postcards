Three tinkers arrived at the marsh with a cart of tiny brass bells and no plan at all. By noon they had tied one bell to every hollow reed, each tuned a little differently. When the wind came, the whole fen hummed a tune nobody had written. A heron paused to listen. A frog requested an encore. The tinkers sat on the cart, pleasantly surprised, and agreed that starting small and trying things side by side had worked far better than any plan would have.
