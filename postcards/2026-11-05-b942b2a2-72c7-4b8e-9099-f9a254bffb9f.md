In the dune town of Saltwhistle, the telegraph office had been murmuring the same half-message for a week. Every evening a chorus of crickets crowded the wire and sang over it, kindly, loudly, endlessly. At last the postmistress, a tortoise named Odile, politely asked them to rehearse in the cactus garden instead. The line went quiet. The message arrived whole before her tea cooled: *Come for supper. Bring the good jam.* The whole street went, slowly, and the crickets sang them there.
