The village kites went up in pairs this morning, each one tied to its twin by a long red ribbon. All but one pair, whose ribbon had slipped loose somewhere in the hedges overnight. A small girl named Odile found it tangled in the blackberries, wound it around her wrist, and ran it back up the hill. By noon the two kites were wheeling together again, and the whole common cheered as if it had been planned that way.
