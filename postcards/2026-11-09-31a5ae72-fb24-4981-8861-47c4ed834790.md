Up on the windy hill, the badgers finally opened the attic everyone had been avoiding. Inside: kites with no string, umbrellas for weather that never came, a hundred jars labelled *maybe later*. They held each one up to the skylight. Anything still humming went back on the shelf. Anything quiet got a polite bow and a ride down the chute. By dusk the floorboards showed again, and the eldest badger lay on them, listening to all that new room breathing.
