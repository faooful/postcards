At dawn the tortoise surveyors found tracks across the salt flat and spent the whole morning arguing over whose they were. The camels swore they had walked single file. The sand foxes said they had tiptoed. In the end a beetle with a magnifying glass pointed out that half the prints had been politely brushed away by the very creatures who made them, hoping to be noticed. Everyone laughed, drew a new map, and agreed to stamp their feet properly next time.
