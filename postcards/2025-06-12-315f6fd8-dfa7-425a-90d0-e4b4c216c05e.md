On the marsh's wire-laced boardwalk, the switchboard frogs keep every conversation hopping to its right lily pad. Tonight they learned a gentler order: a tap of the foot carried each caller forward around the circle of pads, and a double-tap carried them back, wrapping softly past midnight without dropping a single call. The marsh kept chatting, one smooth hop at a time.
