The cliff town of Dunehollow keeps its business on a tall chalk ledge, and every morning the prairie dogs argue over who owes whom a greeting. This week the eldest pup took a bundle of pegs and hung one small flag beside each burrow that still awaited a reply — amber for the neighbors holding a question, plain grey for those merely quiet. By noon the whole cliff could see, without a single shout, which doors deserved a visit first, and the wind did the rest of the announcing.
