At the caravanserai where sandgrouse carry messages between courtyards, the night porter kept a slate by the gate: whenever a winged courier paused mid-journey, the slate now shows exactly what the courier carries — a question, its choices, or the errand awaiting a yes — so travelers decide without chasing the bird down. Two chalk columns grew by morning: one for approvals, one for questions, and nobody mistook the two again. The couriers, visibly relieved, delivered their cargo unopened, as promised.
