On Gullhaven Ridge the weathervanes hold an election every dawn. For years each one spun two arrows at once, which made the vote noisy and nobody could tell which wind had won. This morning a young vane folded its spare arrow away and simply *pointed*, one calm direction with a small brass flag beneath. The others leaned in, considered it, and one by one did the same. The goats on the hillside, who had always been confused, finally knew which way to graze.
