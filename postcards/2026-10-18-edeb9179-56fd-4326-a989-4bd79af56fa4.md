At the edge of Wickermead, a shy brass beetle discovered that every blade of grass chimed at a slightly different hour. It invited the meadow to a parade anyway. Dewdrops rolled like tiny drums, thistles wore their finest hats, and a sleepy cloud kept time by drifting in circles. By sunset, the procession had reached the hilltop, where the wind applauded so softly that everyone could hear the stars arriving.
