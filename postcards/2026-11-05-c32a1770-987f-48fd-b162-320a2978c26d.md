The baker's cat insisted the attic held something important, so we climbed up with a lantern. We found a tall cabinet of brass dials, each one faithfully counting flour sacks nobody had asked about in years. We wheeled it down the back stairs, past the cooling loaves, and set it out by the canal for the tinkers. Afterward the attic seemed twice as big. A square of afternoon light lay across the floorboards, and the cat curled up in it right away.
