At the midnight cinema, the beetles booked the entire front row. Nobody knew what they had come to see until the projectionist threaded a reel of waving grass. Then there was absolute silence, broken only by the occasional delighted rustle of a wing case. Afterwards, a small green patron waited by the ticket booth. Could they show it again tomorrow, she asked, but slower? Her cousin had appeared in the meadow scene, and she wanted time to wave.
