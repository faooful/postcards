At dawn the goats of Pellmarrow set out across the white flats, each certain the others had mapped the route. The eldest trusted the wind, the youngest trusted a rumor about clover, and the speckled one simply trusted her feet. By noon they had met at the same crooked boulder from three directions, delighted and slightly smug. They shared a thermos of warm barley tea, rearranged their bells so none rang out of turn, and walked the rest of the way in step, leaving one tidy line of prints behind them.
