In the glass greenhouse above the fern valley, the moths decided the rafters had grown too crowded. Banners, bunting, little brass dials nobody remembered hanging. So one drizzly evening they carried it all down, piece by fluttering piece, and stacked it neatly by the potting bench. Morning arrived and the ferns looked up to find *sky* where clutter had been. A snail on the sill declared it the best view in years. The moths, dusty and pleased, napped in the warm light.
