Every autumn the owls of Fernhollow hide one silver acorn in the valley and send the young ones to find it. This year a fog rolled in so thick that the clue posts vanished behind a hedge. So the eldest owl moved the posts into the clearing, then hung little glowing ribbons on every branch where an acorn *might* be. The fledglings swooped from ribbon to ribbon, hooting each time one turned out empty, until a small one found the silver acorn in a puddle and laughed so hard she fell in.
