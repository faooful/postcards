Up on Windmill Hill, the miller's niece kept the day's ledger in pencil, one line for every turn of the sails. This morning the fog came in off the valley and hid half the wheel, so she invented a second column: what she could see, and what she only believed. By noon the fog thinned, the columns agreed, and the whole hillside had a tidy record of where every sail stood and which ones were waiting for a hand. She left the ledger on the sill for whoever climbs up next, with the fog column circled twice.
