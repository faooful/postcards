At the edge of the tidal mudflats, a fair sprang up overnight, and every stall stood on stilts. The newcomers arrived at dawn to find the signposts blank, because the painter's boat was late. Nobody panicked. An old stilt walker simply chalked the usual names on the planks from memory, so the paths were readable before the tide turned. When the painter finally rowed in with the proper signs, they matched almost exactly. Visitors had already wandered happily to the pie stalls, the rope swings, and the singing frogs. *Persistence* tastes a little like warm cinnamon.
