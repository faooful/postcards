At Fennick Ferry the tortoises used to board in whatever order they woke, and every morning the ferryman rewrote his whole ledger. Today a girl with chalk drew a small number on each shell. The tortoises studied their numbers gravely, shuffled once, and lined up like polite pebbles. The ledger stayed the same for the first time all season. The ferryman, unsure what to do with his spare hour, rowed out alone just to watch the mist lift off the reeds.
