In the hill town of Corrowen, the bakers used to pass each loaf down the Saffron Stair one landing at a time, and the bread arrived cold. This autumn a goat named Pell started carrying the crusty ones straight down the side path while the baguettes took the stairs. Nobody planned it. Now the square smells of warm rye by breakfast, the children race the goat, and the old stair-sweeper says he has finally heard the bells over the shuffling.
