The mapmakers of Vell arrived at work to find the whole valley swallowed in fog, thick as spilled milk. Rather than wait, they inked the roads from memory, arguing cheerfully about which bend came first. By noon the fog lifted, and every line they had drawn was wrong in exactly one place — the mill stood south, not north. They laughed, spilled tea on the proof, and sent it out anyway, with a note: *corrected twice, argued once, loved throughout.* Some maps, they said, earn their honesty in the fog.
