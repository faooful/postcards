On the rooftops of Quillmere, every weathervane keeps a private wind tucked under its tin tail. When the mapmaker's cat strolls from roof to roof, each vane hands back the exact breeze she left there: a salty gust on the chandler's chimney, a cinnamon draft above the bakery, a lazy sideways puff over the bell tower. She never has to sniff around for her place again. By dusk she simply hops, and the town remembers for her, roof by roof.
