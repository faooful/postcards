In the canyon village of Ostrel, every glassblower once kept a hat of their own invention, each with its own odd brim and its own crooked peg by the furnace. Then a warm wind came down the cliffs one evening and scattered them all. By lamplight the glassblowers built a single long rack, and found to their delight that any hat sat well on any head. Now they trade hats each dusk, and the furnaces glow a little brighter for it.
