In Little Fjordbreak, the harbor tram used to begin each morning on the lower deck, and riders would climb the spiral stairs one by one, yawning. This week the tramline quietly changed its habit: doors open on the upper deck now, where the gulls sit and the light arrives first. The old stairs remain for anyone who prefers them — painted green, still sturdy, still theirs. The conductor says the coffee cart sold out by seven for the first time in years, and nobody has complained about the climb they no longer make.
