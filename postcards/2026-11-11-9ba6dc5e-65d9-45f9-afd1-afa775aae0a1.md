In the canal town of Vell, each bridge had always insisted on its own paint: one plum, one mustard, one a stubborn stripe of teal. Boatmen lost their way counting colors. This autumn the bridges held a meeting under a drizzle so fine it felt like applause, and after much creaking they chose a single quiet blue. Now the gulls glide straight through town, the boatmen hum, and the teal bridge keeps one secret stripe underneath, just for itself.
