Up in the steam-fogged mountain baths, a crew of badgers spent the night setting a new floor. Every tile was the same calm grey, square and patient, waiting for a picture that would come later. They argued gently about spacing, measured with their whiskers, and swept the grout lines until they shone. By dawn the bathers padded in barefoot and stopped, delighted, at how *even* everything felt underfoot. Somebody will paint the tiles next spring. For now, the floor simply hums.
