At the oasis post office, the camels used to accept letters in perfect silence. Travelers would drop an envelope through the slot and stand there in the dunes, wondering if the sand had simply swallowed it. So the eldest camel began a new custom: a loud, cheerful *thump* of the hoof-stamp, then a slow wink through the window. Now every visitor hears their letter land. Some come back just to post blank pages, for the pleasure of that thump.
