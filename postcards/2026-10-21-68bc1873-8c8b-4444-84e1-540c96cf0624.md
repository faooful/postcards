Up at Pellingford Pass, the three old snowplows woke before dawn and found the switchbacks buried under a surprise October drift. Nobody had asked them to dance, but clearing a road in loops turned out to be easier, so they waltzed: one carved the curve, one swept the shoulder, one circled back for the bits the others missed. By noon the mountain goats were trotting down the middle of the road as if they owned it. The plows parked in a row, steaming quietly, rather pleased.
