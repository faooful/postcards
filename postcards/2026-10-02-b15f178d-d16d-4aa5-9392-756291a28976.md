An elderly tortoise arrived at the desert games carrying a suitcase of woollen socks. Nobody had invited a knitting judge, but the dunes were delighted. By afternoon, every sandy peak wore a different striped heel.

The wind tried to organize a race. This proved difficult: the contestants kept slipping comfortably into naps. Our tortoise awarded the grand ribbon to a particularly sleepy hollow, then settled beside it with her suitcase for a pillow. Even the wind lowered its voice.
