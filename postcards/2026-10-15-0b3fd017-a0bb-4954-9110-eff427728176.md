The tide at Dunmarrow comes in on a timetable nobody has ever seen, so the villagers keep a row of tin buckets by the sea wall and trust their hands. Today a surveyor arrived with a very long ruler and a very short temper. By noon she had stopped measuring and was simply sitting on the wall, letting gulls inspect her sandwich. *Something about the place loosens a person.* By evening she was handing out buckets herself, delighted, hair full of spray, quite certain the sea had agreed to everything.
