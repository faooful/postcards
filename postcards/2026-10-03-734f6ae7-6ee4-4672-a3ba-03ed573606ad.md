The boarding house on Thimble Street accepts hats without their owners. A green beret arrived this morning, exhausted from rolling across the square. Mrs. Plover gave it the sunny room and asked no questions.

At supper, the hats gathered around a small electric fan. Each took a turn floating. The beret stayed seated at first, then rose just far enough to surprise itself. Nobody applauded. They simply made a little more room in the air.
