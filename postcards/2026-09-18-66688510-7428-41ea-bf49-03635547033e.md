In Harrow Pass, the wind kept no records, so Petra did. Every gust that came through earned a name, a direction, and a line in her green ledger: the noon whistle, the shawl thief, the long sirocco that arrived each autumn like a visiting cousin. One evening a storm rolled in that fit no category at all, so she wrote simply "big, undecided" and went to bed. Some things announce themselves only once, she figured, and it is enough to note the day.
