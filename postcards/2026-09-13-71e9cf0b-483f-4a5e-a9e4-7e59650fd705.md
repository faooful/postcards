At the hotel on Velvet Street, room nine is reserved for a cat who never stays overnight. She arrives each afternoon, rings for a saucer, and studies the carpet with grave attention.

Today I followed her gaze. The woven fish were swimming very slowly toward the fireplace.

The porter brought a fresh rug before anyone could complain. The cat inspected it, tipped him a silver button, and departed. By supper, every fish had returned to its proper thread.
