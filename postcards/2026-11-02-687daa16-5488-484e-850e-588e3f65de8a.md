Every autumn the hedgehog circus wakes to find its tents have wandered in the night, scattered across the valley like dropped mittens. Nobody remembers which one held the juggling beetles. This year a small mole arrived with a sewing basket and stitched a tiny name ribbon to each flap: *Upper Meadow, Second Ring*. By breakfast the acrobats strolled straight to their own canvas, humming. The tents still drift, of course. But now each one carries its home along with it.
