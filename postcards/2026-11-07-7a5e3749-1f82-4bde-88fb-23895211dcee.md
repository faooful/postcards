The day crew left the steppe station before the storm peaked, and the barograph ran dry at noon: seven hours of falling pressure, the nib scratching a dry groove into the drum. Standing order says the trace never breaks. Marisol came on at dusk, slid a full ink well beneath the arm mid-stroke, and steadied the pen so it never lifted. The line ran thin for a breath, then dark again — one unbroken sentence from morning's high pressure to whatever the storm confesses tonight.
