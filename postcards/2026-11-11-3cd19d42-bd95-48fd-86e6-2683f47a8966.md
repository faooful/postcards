At the mill in Ashfold, Mireille finishes a striped blanket and, before anyone may praise it, turns the whole thing over. The reverse is all knots and loose tails, and she insists the neighbours inspect that side first. *That's the half that did the work,* she says. The cloth goes hand to hand — two faces of one afternoon — and someone laughs at how unlike each other they look.
