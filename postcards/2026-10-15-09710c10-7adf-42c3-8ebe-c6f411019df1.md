The snails at the depot wear little hats, and every morning someone has to decide which hat goes on which shell. Today a heron took the job. She stacked the hats into neat towers by color, wrote a tidy tag for each one, and then lined the snails up in waves so nobody would bump shells at the gate. By noon the whole platform was humming. One snail asked for a feather. The heron said maybe next week, and he seemed fine with that.
