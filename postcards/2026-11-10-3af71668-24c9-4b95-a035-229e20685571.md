Up on the ridge above Tallow Hollow, the snowmen keep a tally of every sledder who passes. Last week a girl in a mustard scarf turned back halfway, and the eldest snowman, the one with a coal-chip monocle, refused to count her. The others grumbled. At dawn they held a solemn recount, pebbles laid in rows on the crust. Two, not three. A hare hopped by to check their arithmetic and approved. They went back to standing very still, *quietly pleased* to have it right.
