In the hill town of Quillmere, the bell tower had a hundred ropes and no ringer, so the tortoises volunteered. They were slow, but they were patient. Each one learned a single rope by heart: first rope for *yes*, second rope for *not today*. By midsummer, a wandering goat could shout a question at the tower and hear the answer chime back before it finished chewing. Nobody had to climb the stairs anymore. The tortoises sat on their warm stones, listened closely, and rang exactly once.
