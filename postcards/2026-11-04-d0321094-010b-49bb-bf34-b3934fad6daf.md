The frozen marsh held a dance every winter, but this year the herons arrived to find the rink rebuilt overnight by industrious beavers. Their old steps no longer fit. So they gathered what still mattered: one graceful turn, a bow, a pocket of warm reeds. Everything else they let drift off with the snow. By dusk the smallest heron glided a perfect loop across the new ice, and the beavers, pretending not to watch, clapped their tails twice.
