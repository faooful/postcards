In the hill town of Quillbury, the owls who run the night library decided the shelves were too tall to fly between. So they built three rooms instead: one long hall for wandering, one quiet nook with a reading lamp beside each book, and one wall of cubbies where every story waited in its own column. The mice voted for the nook. The bats preferred the cubbies. By dawn, everyone had found a favorite, and nobody had to shout across the stacks anymore.
