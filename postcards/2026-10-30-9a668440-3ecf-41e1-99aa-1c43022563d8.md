At Brindlemoor Quay, the harbor clerks kept their tide signs at the bottom of the chalkboard, where nobody looked until the water was already rising. One breezy morning a gull named Pemberton hopped up and nudged the whole row of signs to the top, right beside the day's greeting. The clerks gasped, then grinned. Boats slid in easier, sailors read the signs over their first cup of cocoa, and the chalkboard felt tidier somehow. Pemberton accepted a sardine and went back to napping on a barrel.
