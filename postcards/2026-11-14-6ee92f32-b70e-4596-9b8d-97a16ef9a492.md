In Quillmere, the snails took the old cobbles of the glassblowers' lane up one by one and set down new ones in their exact places. They swore no one would notice. The glassblowers held their breath through a whole week of drizzle. On market day a hundred feet crossed the lane, and not one person stumbled or even looked down. That night the snails toasted their success with dew, and the moths agreed the shine was just the same.
