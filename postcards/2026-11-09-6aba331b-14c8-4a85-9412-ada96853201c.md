At Pebble Pass the clocktower kept thirteen hours, and nobody minded the extra one until the doves began arriving a minute early for their lessons. Bramble, a retired avalanche dog with a whistle she had forgotten how to blow, climbed the stairs each morning and nudged the smallest hand by a whisker. The bells learned a new pause; the snow kept its own counsel; and the extra hour became the town's favorite, spent doing absolutely nothing with great ceremony.
