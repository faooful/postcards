By midafternoon the marsh sky was a knot of strings: red kites, striped kites, kites with bells, all shouting at once. A heron in a woollen scarf waded out, sighed, and began. She tied the loud ones low and let the quiet ones climb. She hung one small ribbon on any kite that truly needed help. By dusk the reeds could see the clouds again, and the frogs, who had been squinting for hours, *finally* applauded.
