On the Glass Archipelago, the tide clerks keep a tiny brass dial on every pier, showing how full the harbor has grown. For years visitors simply guessed, and boats bumped gently into one another. Then a clerk named Wren taught the dial to read only what the water truly said, and to stay dark when the sea was silent. Nobody missed the guessing. The gulls approved, the ferries queued politely, and the harbor felt, for one bright afternoon, wonderfully *roomy*.
