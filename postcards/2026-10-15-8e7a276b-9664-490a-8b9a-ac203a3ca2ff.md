The circus at Brindle Ford had sold every ticket and then the drizzle arrived, so the hedgehog ushers went to work. They nudged the benches into a gentle crescent, set the lamplit ring exactly in the middle, and tucked the bandstand to one side where the tuba could see everyone. Nobody was told to move; the seats simply seemed to be where they belonged. When the acrobats tumbled out, every small face was already turned the right way, dry and *delighted*.
