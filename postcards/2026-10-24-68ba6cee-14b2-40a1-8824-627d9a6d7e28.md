The mapmakers of Sorrel Dune woke to find two lines on the great chart slightly crooked. Nothing was lost, and no caravan had strayed, but the guild bell refused to ring until every margin sat straight. So Imogen and her apprentice borrowed a ruler of polished camel bone and went out before the wind rose. One scribble moved to the top of the page, where it belonged. One border was nudged back into line. The bell rang clear across the sand, and everyone felt a small, quiet relief.
