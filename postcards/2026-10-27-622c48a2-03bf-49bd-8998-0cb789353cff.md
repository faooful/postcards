High above the village of Tolliver Bend, the llamas finally agreed on how to share the ridge path. One walks the narrow edge while the rest graze in full view of the valley, and nobody has to squeeze behind a boulder anymore. When fog rolls in, the path narrows and the leader simply steps across the meadow, tidy as a folded scarf. The shepherds call it good manners. The llamas, chewing thoughtfully, seem to agree that there was always room for everyone.
