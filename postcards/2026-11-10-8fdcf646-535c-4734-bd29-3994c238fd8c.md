Up on the heather ridge, the beekeepers found one hive humming without a name painted on its lid. Nobody could say whose it was, so the bees took offense and buzzed in slow, sulky circles. A girl with a stub of chalk climbed the stile, listened at the wood for a while, and wrote down what the hum seemed to be saying. By moonrise the bees had settled, pleased to be called something again, and the whole ridge smelled faintly of honey and relief.
