In the canal town of Wobbleford, the ferry parrot greeted every passenger with the same word, and every passenger nodded politely, unsure whether it had spoken at all. One rainy morning a small girl tapped its perch and asked for anything else, please. The parrot puffed, thought very hard, and announced *marmalade*. The whole ferry cheered. Now it rings a little brass bell while it thinks, so everyone knows a new word is coming, and waits, grinning, for the surprise.
