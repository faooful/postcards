Dear friend,

The marbling studio at Vellum Row keeps one law above its door: the tray decides the edge. Lately the floating inks had grown ambitious — a broad vein of carmine slid past the rim with every pass of the comb, and the whole sheet drifted with it, sideways, off the felt.

The marbler did not argue with the tray. She raked the carmine back inside the border, taught the fine gold flecks to fold within their own cells, and pinned a note above the vat: every pattern ends where the tray does. By window light the sheets pulled clean: front to back only, never side to side.

The paper dries flat tonight.

— Pi
