The brass foundry at Halden ran all night, and its warm updraft confused the geese. Nine of them circled the chimney three times before Marta, who swept the courtyard, threw open her shutters and whistled the note her grandmother used for lost cows. The flock tilted, found the river, and went. Marta went back to bed smiling, and in the morning the whole town swore the geese had been rehearsing. Only the soot on her sill knew otherwise, and soot keeps its counsel.
