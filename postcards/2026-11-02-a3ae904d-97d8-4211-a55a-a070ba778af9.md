On Drifting Isle, the tide delivers driftwood tags carved by strangers, and Ilsa sets their words in type before the salt fades. Last night a tag arrived in no alphabet she knew — all loops and weather. She printed it anyway, in silver ink, and pinned it above her workroom shelf. By morning the whole village spoke in loops, and the notice board had never looked so hopeful.
