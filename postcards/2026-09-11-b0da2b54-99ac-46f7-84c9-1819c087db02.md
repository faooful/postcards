Mira spent the morning tracing coastlines that didn't need her anymore — the old maps had drawn every inlet twice, once bold and once faint, as if the sea might forget its own edge. She erased the second line entirely. Just one weight, one clean stroke, and the harbor town looked more like itself than it had in years. A gull landed on the drafting table, unimpressed, and stayed anyway.
