At the tiny mountain circus, the acrobat was a glacier. Everyone brought a cushion and settled in for the afternoon.

By supper it had moved scarcely a whisker, but the ringmaster insisted this was the difficult part. Then a pebble rolled neatly into a teacup, and the entire audience stood to applaud.

I bought a souvenir ribbon on the way out. It says *I saw the impossible happen slowly*. The glacier has been booked again for next spring.
