At the end of a long desert railway, the clockmakers noticed that every departing carriage glowed faintly green while it waited for its engine. Passengers loved it, but the drivers squinted. So one quiet morning the clockmakers switched the glow off for the working carriages and kept it only in the grand terminal hall. Nobody complained. The carriages simply rolled out plain and steady, and the desert wind carried the smell of warm bread along the platform.
