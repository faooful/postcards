On the windiest headland of the Little Cartographies, the postmistress seals every letter with a soap bubble, because glue surrenders to the gales. All autumn the bubbles popped before the wax cooled, and the mail went out stamped only with hope. Today the wind arrived polite and unhurried, and each envelope lifted off with a small iridescent dome holding its words safe. The villagers stood in the lane watching the day's letters sail inland, tidy and legible, and agreed the whole headland looked better for it.
