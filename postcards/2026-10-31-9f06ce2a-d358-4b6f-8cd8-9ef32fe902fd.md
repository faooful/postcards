Down in the valley of Ampersand, two rivers that had always run to separate seas finally agreed on one map. The cartographer's guild spent an afternoon inking the junction, then walked the whole shoreline twice to be sure no village was printed twice and none left off. By dusk, every ferryman had a copy, and the delta glowed with the quiet of things that finally agree.
