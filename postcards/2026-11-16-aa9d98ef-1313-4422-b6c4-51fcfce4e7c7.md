The pass above Corrie Moss stayed shut for weeks, and the badgers grew tired of shouting messages across the drifts. Every morning they dug a little further, swapping shovels and arguments in equal measure.

Today the last snowbank slumped, and a sledge of letters slid straight into the square. The eldest badger rang the bell so hard her hat flew off. Nobody chased it. Everyone was too busy opening envelopes that had waited all winter to say *hello, finally.*
