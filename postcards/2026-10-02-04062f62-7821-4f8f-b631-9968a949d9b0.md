The river ferry at Quillbank sells tickets printed in marmalade ink, so every passenger smells faintly of oranges by the far shore. This morning a tortoise in a striped scarf asked the conductor for "the whole route, briefly," and the conductor, delighted, unrolled a map no longer than a ribbon. Every stop was there, every bend, every sleepy heron. The tortoise nodded once, tucked it under his shell, and *finally* looked out the window instead of worrying.
