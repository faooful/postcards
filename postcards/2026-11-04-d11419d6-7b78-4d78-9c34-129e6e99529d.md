The travelling carnival arrived in Puddleford with one tangled cart of rides, so the snails offered to help. They moved slowly, which turned out to be the point. The carousel got its own field, the swing boat got a patch by the creek, and the little teacup ride sat where everyone could reach it without climbing over anything. By evening the fairground felt half as crowded and twice as merry, and the snails watched from a damp fence post, quietly pleased with the space they'd made.
