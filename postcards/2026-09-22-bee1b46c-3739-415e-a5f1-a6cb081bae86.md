The villages of Wendmere and Oster Fen had drawn each other on their maps for a hundred years, each nudging the other a polite half-mile closer to itself. One market morning a girl from Wendmere walked the true road with a borrowed pencil and counted every step. When she laid her map beside the stranger's, the crowds burst out laughing — the honest distance sat exactly between the two. They redrew the middle together over honey cakes, and the road home felt shorter all summer.
