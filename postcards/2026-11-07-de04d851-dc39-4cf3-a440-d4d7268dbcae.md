The frost came early to the plum orchard, so the hedgehogs worked all night. They rolled warm stones from the bakery ovens up the hill, one row of trees at a time, tucking each stone among the roots and hurrying back for more. Twice the path iced over and they had to start a new route around the pond. By dawn every blossom was still pink. The baker found them asleep in a flour sack, *very* pleased with themselves.
