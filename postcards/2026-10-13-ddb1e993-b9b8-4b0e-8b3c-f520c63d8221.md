Every dawn the otters of Wenlow Reach haul the floating mailbag onto a warm rock and argue about what matters first. A soggy invitation to a pebble-skipping duel goes to the top. Seventy-odd polite notes about other otters' dams go in a tidy pile marked *later, maybe*. The eldest otter insists the heap was much taller yesterday, back when everyone's chores were mixed in with your own. Now the rock fits one small, honest stack, and there is still time left for a swim.
