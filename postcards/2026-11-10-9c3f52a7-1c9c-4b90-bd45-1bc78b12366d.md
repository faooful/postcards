Every kite at the quarry festival came out of its box the same shade of blue, and the children were sure someone had stolen the paint. A stonecutter climbed the tallest ledge to look. The paint was all there, she reported, every jar full. The trouble was the order: each child reached for the first jar on the shelf. By noon they had shuffled the jars, and the sky over the white stone turned into a patchwork.
