The funicular up Mount Querrow stalls halfway every afternoon, and nobody minds. Today a hive of travelling bees took the front car, and their keeper, a tall woman in a straw veil, hummed to keep them calm. When the cable lurched, the bees did not scatter. They simply rose together, circled once over the conductor's cap, and settled back exactly where they had been. Everyone applauded. The conductor tipped his cap to the bees, and the hill gave us a slow, honeyed sunset.
