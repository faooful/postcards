This morning the shoal maps were a drift of unmarked water, so the mussel cartographers called a modest council on the warm flats. Each family's currents went into its own chart: one for the busy channel, one for the quiet backwater, one for the survey skiff. Busy ink-swarm days were folded into a slim appendix nobody has to open. By afternoon the tide could find any errand in a glance. The council adjourned early, pleased, and let the water decide the rest.
