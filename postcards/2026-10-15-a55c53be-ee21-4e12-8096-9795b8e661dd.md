At dusk on Saltmere Flats the moths unpack their paper kites, each one painted a different soft colour. This year the wind arrived early and in a good mood, so every kite found its own lane of sky without bumping a neighbour. A small grey moth kept her spool tucked at the corner of the field, ready for anyone with a question. Nobody needed much help. The kites hummed, the salt glittered, and the whole crowd agreed the evening looked exactly as it had been imagined.
