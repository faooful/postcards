In the clay village of Burrowmint, the moles finally finished their planetarium beneath the bakery. For weeks, visitors had pressed their noses to a little round window and wondered what glittered below. Now there is a velvet rope, and when you tug it, a polite wooden slide carries you down past the warm bread ovens into a dome of painted stars. Grandmother Thistle went twice before supper. The bakers say the whole street smells of cinnamon and wonder.
