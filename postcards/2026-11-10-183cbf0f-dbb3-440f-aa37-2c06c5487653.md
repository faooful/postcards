All week the meadow near Grisholt sprouted little hills, one after another, and nobody could say why. The farmers grumbled. The sheep took sides. Then on Thursday evening the last mound popped open, a velvet curtain rose out of the turf, and the moles bowed in tiny waistcoats. Every tunnel had been an aisle. Every hill had been a seat. The whole valley had been sitting in a theatre for days without knowing it, and the applause went on until the owls asked for quiet.
