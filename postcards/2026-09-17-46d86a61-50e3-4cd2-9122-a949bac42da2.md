The municipal swimming pool in Brindlewick has filled with velvet this week. Nobody knows why, but the diving instructor has adapted beautifully. She teaches the children to tumble, then awards ribbons for especially graceful landings. An elderly gentleman still arrives every morning in his bathing cap and completes twelve solemn lengths on foot. Today he invited me into the shallow end. We stood ankle-deep in burgundy folds while the lifeguard blew a soft whistle, simply to keep the afternoon in order.
