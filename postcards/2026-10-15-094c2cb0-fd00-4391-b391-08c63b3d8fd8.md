At Dunmarrow Hollow, the evening bell rang every dusk, yet no villager ever heard it. The foxes who lived beneath the tower grew puzzled, then curious, then very patient. They climbed the stairs one by one, sniffing at corners, until the youngest found a wool sock stuffed deep inside the bell, left behind after some forgotten experiment. Nobody was scolded. The foxes simply set the sock in a basket nearby, in case anyone wished to keep it, and waited for dusk. When the bell finally sang, the whole valley looked up, delighted.
