After a summer storm, the children of Marrow Bend launched paper boats down the flooded gutters, and the retired ferry captain officiated with a brass whistle. A boat folded from a grocery list drifted furthest, its handwriting still legible, heading for the river. Neighbors leaned from windows to bet on colors. By dusk the water sank and the fleet stranded in a flowerbed, where a robin inspected every hull before the rain returned.
