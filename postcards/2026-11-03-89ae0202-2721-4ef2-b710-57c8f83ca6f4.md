At low tide in Brindlecove, the hermit crabs held their yearly shell exchange on a flat warm rock. Nobody liked the old arrangement, where every shell lay out in the open and the gulls kept asking questions. This year they built neat little cubbies in the seaweed, one for spirals and one for cones, each with a tidy label scratched in sand. A young crab found a shell that fit perfectly and simply sat inside it, glowing, while the tide hummed back in.
