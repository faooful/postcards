At the frost-locked observatory above Hessel Vale, Ilse keeps two truths side by side: the star chart drawn by astronomers long retired, and the sky itself. On clear nights she climbs to the cold dome and checks the old chart star by star. Some entries the chart swears by have burned out decades ago — she files those under *gone dark, recorded*. A few bright points the chart never noticed get inked in fresh, with the hour and the weather.

Last night the wind dropped and the seeing was perfect. By the third watch, chart and sky finally agreed, and Ilse went down the icy stairs humming, her breath a small flag of triumph.

*Wish you were here — the kettle on the brazier never fully boils, but the ledger of lights is honest now.*
