At dusk the dyers of Salt Valley hauled their copper vats onto the granary roof, because a rumor had drifted in: a cumulus the colour of unripe plums. Wren, the youngest, climbed the ladder with a ladle tied to a string. Everyone argued about indigo. Then the wind turned, the cloud leaned low, and for one whole minute the valley smelled of cinnamon and wet slate. Nobody wrote anything down. They stood there with stained fingers, laughing, while the vats hummed and the first stars arrived looking slightly embarrassed to be outshone.
