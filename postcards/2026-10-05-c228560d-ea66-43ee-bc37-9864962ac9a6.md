The glass snails of Ondry hold their race each autumn on the greenhouse roof. This year the organizers took down the fancy banners and painted one bright dot on each snail's shell: green for racing, amber for napping, grey for gone to lunch. The crowd adored it. Nobody had to squint. One snail, Pemberton, stopped halfway to admire his own dot in a puddle and came in very last. He seemed delighted anyway, and the rain kindly held off until the trophies were handed out.
