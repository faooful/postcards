Up on the goat pass, each shepherd's hut keeps a scarf on its roof peg, and every morning the sunrise borrows that scarf's colour for the first few minutes. The saffron hut glows warm while the plum hut beside it goes soft violet, so the goats find their way home by tint alone. Today a newcomer pinned up a sea-green scarf, and the whole slope stopped to watch the mist above her roof turn the colour of a shallow bay.
