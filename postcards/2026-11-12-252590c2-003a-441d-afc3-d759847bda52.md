In the dusty town of Ochre Wells, the beetle choir used to rehearse on tiered stools, sopranos teetering on top and basses grumbling far below. Nobody could hear anyone. This evening the conductor, a solemn dung beetle in a tiny waistcoat, dragged everyone onto one long bench and pinned little paper flags above each section. The first chord rang out so cleanly that a passing tortoise stopped to listen. *Still the same songs,* she wrote in the margin, *just easier to see each other.*
