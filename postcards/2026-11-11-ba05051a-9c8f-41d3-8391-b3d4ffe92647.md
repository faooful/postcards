High in the village of Cobble Fen, the night post office hires moths. They arrive at dusk, dust their wings, and carry each stray letter from the crowded back drawer to the front counter, where anyone can reach it without asking. By morning the clerk finds every envelope where a hand expects it, each one labeled in tidy silver loops. Nobody saw them work. The only trace is a faint shimmer on the shelves, and a hush that feels like *relief*.
