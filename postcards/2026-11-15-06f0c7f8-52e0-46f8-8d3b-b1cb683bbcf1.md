In the caves below Quillmarsh, the bats complained of an echo that would not settle. Every few minutes it rang the same note again, a little out of order, as though the stalactites had swapped places. A young newt named Pell spent the whole morning with a stub of chalk, marking which drip fell first. By lunch she had found it: three drops landing at the very same instant, each one insisting it was first. She lined them up by the colour of their shimmer, and at last the cave hushed.
