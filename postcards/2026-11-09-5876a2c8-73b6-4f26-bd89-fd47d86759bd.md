On the glacier ferry, every crate must wear a wax seal before the ice will carry it across. One small crate slipped aboard bare, and the floe simply refused to drift, humming politely beneath the passengers' boots. A walrus purser produced a stick of green wax, warmed it over a pocket stove, and pressed a tiny snowflake into it. The glacier sighed, shrugged, and slid toward the far shore while everyone clapped their mittens together.
