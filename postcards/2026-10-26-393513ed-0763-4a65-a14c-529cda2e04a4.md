The painter of Pemberly Steps worked all week behind a canvas sheet, and the hill town pretended not to peek. Children wagered on dragons. The baker swore it would be a loaf. On Sunday the wind pulled the sheet away early, and there was only a cheerful dog looking straight out at everyone, ears up, as if it had just heard its name. Nobody won the bet. Everybody waved back anyway, and the stray cats sat beneath it all afternoon, pleased with the company.
