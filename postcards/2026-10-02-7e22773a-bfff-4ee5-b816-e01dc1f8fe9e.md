The flood took the tunnel on a Tuesday, and the morning tram to Bindlewood sat sighing at the barrier. Marisol the driver read the closure notice twice, shrugged, and turned her tram up the ridge road, which no timetable had believed in for forty years. Passengers grumbled until the trees parted and the whole valley lay below them, wearing its ordinary fog like a shawl. Everyone arrived nine minutes late and completely changed. The tunnel drained by Thursday, but the ridge road keeps its new schedule now, printed in small triumphant letters.
