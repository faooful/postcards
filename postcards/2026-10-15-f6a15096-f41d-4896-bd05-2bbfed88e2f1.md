The wind arrived sideways this morning, which the kite-flyers of the salt flats consider extremely good manners. By noon every string in town was taut. A small girl's kite kept snagging in the same gust, again and again, until she simply let out more line and it soared over the gust instead. Everyone applauded, including a flock of very opinionated flamingos. Tonight the kites hang from the rafters like sleeping bats, still faintly humming. *Wish you were here to hold a spool.*
