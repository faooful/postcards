High in the hazel wood, the squirrel conductors retired the acorn tram's creaky switchbacks this morning. They oiled the canopy rails, repainted every station sign in acorn-brown, and posted a timetable the hedgehogs could finally read without squinting. By noon the little tram hummed along its old loop looking brand-new, and a dormouse conductor confessed she kept riding past her stop just to hear the fresh bells ring.
