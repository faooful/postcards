At the top of Pellowyn's terraced orchard, three badgers unrolled a very long map and declared that nobody would replant everything in one afternoon. Instead they tied ribbons to the trees: blue for this week, yellow for after the frost, and a cheerful plaid for whenever the moles agreed. A heron wandered by and asked why it was going so slowly. The eldest badger offered her a pear and said, *one terrace at a time, so we can see what blooms*.
