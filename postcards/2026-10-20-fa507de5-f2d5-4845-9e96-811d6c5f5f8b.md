In the hill town of Merrowgate, the glassblowers woke to find their furnace wind had wandered off overnight, humming through laundry lines and rattling the chapel weathervane backwards. They followed it street by street with butterfly nets and patient bellows, coaxing it home past the bakery's open window. By dusk every gust was back in its kiln, and the first bowl of the evening came out clear as a held breath. Somebody rang a little tin bell for no reason at all.
