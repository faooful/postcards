Deep in the warm stacks beneath Mount Ember, the salamanders decided the grand reading hall had grown too crowded with signposts. They carried one gilded placard down to the cellar, then tucked a small brass bell beside the hearth instead. Ring it, and every scroll ever written glides out of the walls at once. The visiting moths were baffled for an afternoon, then delighted. By evening the hall felt roomier, the lava hummed contentedly, and someone hung a new nameplate over the theatre of moving pictures.
