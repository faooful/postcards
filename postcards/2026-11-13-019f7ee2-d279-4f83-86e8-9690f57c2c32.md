The Hollowmere town band discovered it by accident: on the first cold morning, the trumpets sounded sweeter, as if the frost had tuned them overnight. So they marched at five, pajamas under uniforms, playing anything with high notes. The baker waved floury arms from his window. The fishmonger called it a nuisance and quietly set her alarm for the next cold snap. By November the whole street belonged to brass weather, and nobody admitted they had planned to be awake.
