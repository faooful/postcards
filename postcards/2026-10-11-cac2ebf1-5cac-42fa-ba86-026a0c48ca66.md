At dawn the beekeepers of the salt flats discovered their hives had been stacked two rows deep, and nobody could remember why. Marguerite shrugged, slid every second hive up beside its neighbour, and hung a small brass tally on the lonely one at the end. By noon the flats looked tidy and a little proud. A passing camel counted the hives twice, nodded, and went on its way, which Marguerite took as high praise.
