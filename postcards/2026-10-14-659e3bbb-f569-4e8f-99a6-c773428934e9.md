In the village of Quillmarrow, a goat named Pemberton rang the fog bell each dawn, though nobody had ever asked her to. One morning the mist rolled in thick as porridge, and the villagers found they could see only the tops of their own hats. Pemberton didn't mind. She nudged a barrel of salt to the middle of the square, climbed on top, and waited. By noon, everyone had followed the faint jingle, laughing and bumping into one another, and the whole village sat down to a surprisingly delightful lunch.
