The ferrets of Upper Wobbleton decided the town's weather vane belonged on the east side of the chapel, where the wind actually arrived. They carried it across the square at dawn, arguing politely about which end was the rooster. By noon the vane pointed true, the baker could finally read it from her window, and the pigeons took their old perch with only mild complaint. Nothing new was built. Everything simply stood where it made sense, and the whole square seemed to breathe easier.
