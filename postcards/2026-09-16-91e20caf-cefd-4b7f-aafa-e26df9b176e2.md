Dispatch from the junction at Verlorn Halt, where the platform used to hold both the waiting bell and the arrivals board crammed onto one narrow strip of stone. Old Fen, the signalman, finally got his wish this week: the arrivals board now runs the whole length of the platform on its own level, letters wide and unhurried, while the bell and the lantern-switch moved up to the gantry where they belonged all along.

A surveyor came through with a plumb line and found the platform's old measuring marks were painted a hand's width off from where the paving actually started, so the board had been hanging crooked without anyone noticing. Fen re-chalked the line to match the paving stones instead, and now the board sits flush with the rest of the platform, easy to read from the road.

Weather held. The 4:10 came in on time.
