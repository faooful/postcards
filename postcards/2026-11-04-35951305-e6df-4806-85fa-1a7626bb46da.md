Dear shore,

At Thumb Cove the tide dropped far enough today to expose the long shelf of pools, and the colour wardens came out with their little swatch cards. For seasons the pools had been wearing whatever greens and violets the souvenir stands sold, each jar mixed by a different stand. The wardens held each card against the water, shook their heads, and poured the true sea-colours in from a single shared flask — one green for "awake," one violet for "settled," one gray for "not yet." By the evening ferry the whole shelf matched, pool to pool, the way the sea itself does.

The wardens stayed to watch the light settle on the matching water, and went home pleased.
