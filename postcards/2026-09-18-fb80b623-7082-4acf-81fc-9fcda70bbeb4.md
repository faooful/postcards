Every October the town drains the municipal pool, and by November the brass band moves in. Odette carries her tuba down the concrete steps at first light, and the empty basin gives every note a second, rounder echo. Today the fog arrived early and sat in the deep end like a polite guest, so the music went everywhere at once, soft and enormous. A jogger stopped mid-stride. A baker opened her window. Nobody said a word; some mornings are simply agreed upon.
