The herons of Marrowfen hold their census every autumn, wading slowly through the reeds and counting each stalk twice, once with each leg. This year a heron named Tolliver noticed that one patch of reeds had been counted by two different herons, both very sure of themselves. Nobody was wrong, exactly. They just held a long, polite meeting on a mudbank, ate a few minnows, and agreed to mark the counted reeds with a small blue ribbon. The fen looked festive for weeks.
