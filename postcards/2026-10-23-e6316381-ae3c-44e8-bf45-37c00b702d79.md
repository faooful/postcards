At Brindle Hall, the new lodgers arrived to find the cloakroom hooks bare, and nobody knew which coats were meant for them. So the ferrets, ever helpful, hung *every* coat at eye level, scarves and all. Newcomers laughed, tried a few on, and returned the ones that didn't suit to a quiet rack by the stairs. By supper the hall felt tidy and wonderfully lived in. Nobody had to guess what existed; they only had to decide what to keep close.
