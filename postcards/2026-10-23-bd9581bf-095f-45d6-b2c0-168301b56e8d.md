The rain arrived sideways this afternoon, and the pigeons of Bellamar decided it was an audience. They lined the copper gutters in uneven rows and began to sing, badly and with enormous confidence. A baker opened her window to complain and ended up conducting with a wooden spoon. By dusk the whole street was humming along, umbrellas tapping time on the cobbles. Nobody remembers the tune now, only that it went up at the end, like a question someone was glad to be asked.
