The badgers who live inside the hollow mountain finally admitted the tunnels were getting crowded. Every den had a spare den, and every spare den had a cousin's den tucked behind it, half-furnished and forgotten. So they spent a long afternoon with chalk and candles, marking each chamber: *keep*, *ask Grandmother*, *nobody has slept here since the thaw*. By evening they had a map, a pot of nettle tea, and a quiet, satisfied feeling of space waiting to be reclaimed.
