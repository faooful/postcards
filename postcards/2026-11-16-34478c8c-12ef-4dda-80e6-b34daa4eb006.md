In the hill town of Quessaly, the glassblowers have one rule: never blow twice before the first bubble settles. This morning an apprentice named Tibb forgot. Two vases puffed up at once, wobbled across the workbench and tipped onto a sleeping cat, who woke wearing a very surprised green hat. Nobody scolded Tibb. The old masters just tapped the hourglass on the windowsill and waited, the way you wait out a sneeze. By noon the vases had cooled into perfect pears, and the cat refused to give its hat back.
