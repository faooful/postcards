The wind over the pumpkin quarry blew every direction at once this morning, so the kite flyers tied each kite to its own small stake and let the sky arrange them. Box kites drifted beside diamond kites, and a striped dragon unfurled near a plain paper square. From the rim you could step back and see the whole flock, or lean close and count every stitch. Nobody chased a string. By noon the grandmothers were naming them all.
