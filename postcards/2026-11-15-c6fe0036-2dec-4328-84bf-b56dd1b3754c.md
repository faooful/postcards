In the copper dunes of Vellaric, the sand freezes overnight into a thin glittering crust. Every morning a troupe of hedgehogs straps on tiny snowshoes and checks each dune for hollow spots before the caravans cross. Today they found two: one that sang when tapped, and one that only pretended to be solid. They marked both with ribbon flags, drank warm cardamom milk, and argued cheerfully about which flag was prettier. The caravans will travel safely tomorrow.
