The travelling show arrived at Quillimoor in six painted caravans, each one unable to unpack until the one ahead had pitched its poles. Petra, aged nine, ran between them with a stick of blue chalk, drawing arrows in the grass so nobody parked in the wrong order. By moonrise the accordion wagon leaned on the puppet wagon, which leaned on the bakery, and the whole ring stood up at once like a held breath let go. Everyone clapped for the chalk.
