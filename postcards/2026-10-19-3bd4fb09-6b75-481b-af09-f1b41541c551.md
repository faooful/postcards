Three tortoises set out across the shimmering salt flats with a single chalk line and a great deal of patience. Each one walked a little ahead of the last, checking that the line still pointed somewhere sensible. Twice they found a spot where the chalk curved toward a mirage, and they stopped to argue kindly about it over warm pebbles. By dusk the line was mostly straight, two bends were circled for tomorrow, and the wind carried the faint smell of rain from very far away.
