The moths of the glass conservatory rehearsed their opera three times before the storm arrived. The first time the ferns sang too loudly. The second time a snail wandered across the stage and everyone politely waited. By the third attempt the thunder had settled into a low drumroll, rain tapped the panes in perfect time, and the smallest moth finally hit her high note. The orchids applauded with a slow, fragrant *whoosh*, and nobody minded that the curtain was a damp leaf.
