At the edge of Quillfen Marsh, the beetles had a problem: the welcome banner kept sliding down the reed whenever anyone climbed past it. Visitors arrived at the top, squinted around, and never found the entrance.

So the smallest beetle, Orrin, tied the banner to a dragonfly's wing. Now it rode alongside every climber, bright and level, no matter how high or low they went. The visitors stopped squinting. Someone clapped. A moth asked if the banner could also carry snacks, and Orrin said maybe next season.
