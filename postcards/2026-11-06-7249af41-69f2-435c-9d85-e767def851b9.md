Out on the salt flat, the raccoons used to flash a hundred little mirrors at once, and nobody could tell which glint meant *supper* and which meant *storm*. So they climbed onto three tall stools, one raccoon per hill, and agreed that only the stool-sitters would signal. Everyone else simply waved up at them. Tonight the sky is calm, the mirrors are tidy in their crates, and one clear flash says the caravan is coming home with figs.
