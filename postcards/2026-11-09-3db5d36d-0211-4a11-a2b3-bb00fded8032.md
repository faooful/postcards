For nine days the canal under Lantern Row lay perfectly still, because two ferrymen each believed the other held the only oar. Nobody had seen either of them since the frost. This morning a girl with a lamp walked the towpath, found one ferry moored in the wrong basin, and politely untied it. By noon the water was moving again, carrying sleepy barges and a brass band that had been waiting since autumn. The new ferryman hums *as he rows*, and the gulls have stopped arguing.
