On the slow ferry across Thimble Lake, a crew of badgers decided every boat needed a small flag that said who was steering. They painted them in tangerine and plum, then argued gently about whether the ducklings trailing behind deserved flags too. *No,* said the eldest, *ducklings follow, they don't steer.* By dusk the lake shimmered with tidy little banners, and the gulls, for once, knew exactly whom to pester for crackers.
