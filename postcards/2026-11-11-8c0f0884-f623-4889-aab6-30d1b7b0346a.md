At the Varrow night market, the stall signs hang on one long wire, and every spring the wind pushes them a hand's width lower than the lamplight reaches. Old Pell, who sells nothing but *directions*, decided this year to count the awning too. She measured it twice, lifted the wire, and suddenly the last sign in the row sat exactly where a tired shopper's eye lands. Nobody noticed, which is how she likes it. The soup seller said the whole street felt more *honest* somehow, and bought a direction he did not need.
