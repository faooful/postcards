The abandoned bathhouse hosted its first snail race this evening. Someone chalked a finish line across the tiles, and the spectators settled into the empty tubs with cushions. By midnight, the favorite had stopped to admire a drain. Nobody minded. The announcer whispered updates through a rolled towel while a beetle sold tiny pennants. When the last racer finally crossed, applause pattered softly against the porcelain. Every contestant received a ribbon long enough to sleep beneath.
