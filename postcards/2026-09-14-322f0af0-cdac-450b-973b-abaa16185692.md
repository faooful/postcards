The corridor used to have one door with three hallways crammed behind it, everyone bumping elbows trying to get past. A quiet carpenter came through this week with a folding rule and no fuss, and now there are three doors, each opening straight onto its own room, sunlit and uncluttered. The old room stays too, rearranged so the two busiest baskets of mail sit right by the entrance instead of buried in a drawer. Down the hall, someone's collection of little painted figures on a shelf is undisturbed, still lined up exactly as they left them. A cat wandered through to inspect the new doors, approved, and left a single muddy pawprint as a signature.
