In Lantern Gully the bees travel in small accordion bands. One bee always flies in front, humming the tune, and the rest fold neatly behind her like pleats. The villagers used to see only the leader and assume she was alone. This autumn the bands began stitching a tiny glowing tally beneath her wings, so everyone could tell how many more were tucked behind. Now, when a band drifts past the bakery, children count the pleats aloud, and the music sounds fuller for being known.
