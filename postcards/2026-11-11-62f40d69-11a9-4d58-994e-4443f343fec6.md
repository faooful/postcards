High in the Velloway pass, the marmot choir rehearses in a cave that answers back. For weeks every call came home tangled: the smallest squeak arrived as loud as the warning whistle, and nobody knew which to heed. This morning the eldest marmot moved one pebble ledge and asked everyone to hum softer except the lookout. Now the urgent note rings first and clear, the gossip settles into a pleasant murmur behind it, and the whole valley finally hears what matters.
