In the canyon town of Mossreach, a little brass bell hangs halfway up the waterfall, and for years nobody could reach it. Visitors waved at it politely and went home. This week a family of otters strung a rope of braided reeds from the ledge, and now anyone who tugs it is carried gently along a mist-slick slide into the hidden pool behind the falls. Children ring it, grandmothers ring it, one very serious goose rang it twice. The water sounds pleased.
