Every Thursday the pigeons of Vellmarsh Square hold their chess tournament on the fountain's rim. The rules are strict and a little silly: after any bird moves, everyone must coo once before the next peck. Young Bartholomew hated it. He pecked twice in his excitement and knocked his own bishop into the water. Now he coos with great dignity, puffs his chest, and waits. The crowd of sparrows agrees the games are slower but much fairer. Nobody has lost a bishop to the fountain since.
