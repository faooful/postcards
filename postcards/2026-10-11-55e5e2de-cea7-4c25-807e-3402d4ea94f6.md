In the canal town of Vell, the lanterns along the water had always been plain jars with a wick. This autumn the glassblowers rowed out at dusk and gave each one a rim of pale green, a little collar, a soft glow that rippled when a barge passed. Nobody asked them to. The ducks noticed first, then the bakers, then a boy who swore the lantern by the bridge was *humming*. By midnight the whole canal looked dressed for a wedding no one had announced.
