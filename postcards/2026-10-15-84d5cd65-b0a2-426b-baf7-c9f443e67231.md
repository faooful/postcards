Dear friend, the owls of the cedar wood held their yearly count last night. Each one flew a slow loop over every hollow and burrow, noting which paths had grown tangled and which signposts pointed at nothing. A badger complained that his front step was too narrow for visitors; the owls wrote it down with great seriousness. By dawn they had a long list, sorted with a little ceremony, and the wood felt lighter just for being looked at so carefully.
