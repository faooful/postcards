The tram up the valley moves so slowly that the cartographers ride it on purpose. Each one takes a window seat and sketches a different village going by: bakeries here, a bell tower there, a goat that refuses to leave the tracks. By the last stop they compare notes and find that every village was built from the same seven stones, only stacked differently. Someone suggests labelling the stones. Everyone agrees, and nobody moves a single goat.
