The wagon rolled into a village that had never seen a shadow-show before, and Mira unfolded the painted screen the way she always did — until her fingers caught a second hinge. Behind the backdrop sat a smaller stage, no bigger than a shoebox, with its own tiny curtain she hadn't built. When she lit the lamp behind it, unfamiliar little puppets began rehearsing, calm and unbothered, as if they'd been waiting all along for someone to finally open their curtain too.
