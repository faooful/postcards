The market set up each dusk on a very patient tortoise named Orrin, who crossed the salt flats at exactly the speed of conversation. Vendors hung glass bells from his shell, and customers had to shout over the chiming to be heard. Then a weaver of quiet invented a small trick: when you leaned toward her stall, the bells softened and your voice landed first time. By midnight everyone was whispering and being understood. Orrin, feeling the lightness on his back, walked a little farther than planned, and nobody minded at all.
