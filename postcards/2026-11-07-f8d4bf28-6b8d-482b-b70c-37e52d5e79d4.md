At first light the harbor master tried her new brass whistle set — one to nine, each pitch a boat. The old rule only roused the vessels already under way; the sleeping trawlers, the moored dinghies, even the little oyster barge nobody remembered naming sat silent at their lines. This morning she blew them all in order, and the whole fleet woke together, bells clanking, gulls scandalized, nine bows swinging toward open water. The town called it the finest head count in living memory.
