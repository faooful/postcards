At the old windmill post office in Whetmills, the mice work the night shift. When the meteor shower began, every lamp in the village went dark on purpose — the sky was bright enough to read envelopes by. Pip and her cousins sorted the midnight mail on the roof tiles, matching each letter's looping handwriting to a doorstep from memory. One parcel, addressed simply "to whoever needs it," took three guesses. By dawn the last stamp was licked, and the whole village woke to find its small wishes answered right on time.
