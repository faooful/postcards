A flower outside the cobbler’s shop refused to bloom until its feet were comfortable. Nobody had previously considered its feet. The cobbler knelt beside the stem with a tape measure and a very serious expression.

By evening, twelve velvet slippers stood neatly around the flowerpot. The petals opened one at a time, revealing a deep purple centre and what appeared to be considerable relief.

The cobbler left the bill beneath a leaf. Payment arrived in pollen, which he accepted without counting.
