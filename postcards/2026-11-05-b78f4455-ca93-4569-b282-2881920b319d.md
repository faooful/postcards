By the reedy start line of the Marshwater Regatta, the otters found every raft tied together in one jolly heap, and nobody could tell which raft had been built by whom. So the eldest otter fetched a basket of bright thread and began stitching tiny tags onto the sails of the rafts each family had made with their own paws. The borrowed rafts stayed plain. By noon, the herons judging the race nodded approvingly, and the youngest otter waved proudly at a sail that said, quite simply, *ours*.
