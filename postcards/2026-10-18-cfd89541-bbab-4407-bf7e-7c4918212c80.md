In Quillwick Hollow the hillside cottages used to crowd together like sleepy sheep, every window shouting for attention. This week the villagers strung a single ribbon of little flags along the lane, one for each house, and now travellers simply follow their favourite colour to the right door. Only one cottage glows at a time, warm and unhurried. Wanderers *hum* as they pick a flag, and the kettles seem to hum back. It is wonderfully calm, and nobody has lost their way since.
