At Tamsin Ford the river stones used to chatter in long run-on sentences, so the herons never knew where to step. This autumn the stones agreed to sit in tidy rows, each wearing a small painted collar saying who it was and which way it leaned. The herons crossed in record time and were faintly disappointed nobody fell in. A badger set up a stall selling warm acorn tea to anyone who reached the far bank dry. The water seemed relieved, and hummed all the way to the mill.
