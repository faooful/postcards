On the slow green river below Pebblewick, three otters run a ferry made of one wide lily pad. Ring the reed bell once and they paddle over. Ring it again and they drift politely back to the bank, no fuss at all. This morning a heron tried to board by stepping on the ferry's edge. The otters only tipped their caps and waited for him to take the proper plank. By noon everyone had crossed dry, and the river hummed along, *pleased with itself*.
