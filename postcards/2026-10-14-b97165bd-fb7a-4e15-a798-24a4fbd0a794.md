This morning three otters in waistcoats paddled into the reed labyrinth with clipboards, checking each bend for anything amiss. They found a heron napping across one channel, a snail who had painted every signpost the same shade of green, and a frog insisting a dead end was "a scenic pause." Each discovery was noted with great seriousness and a small splash. By noon the reeds swayed approvingly, and the otters floated home on their backs, *very* pleased with their tidy little report.
