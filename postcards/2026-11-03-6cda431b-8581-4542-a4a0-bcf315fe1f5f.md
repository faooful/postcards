On the reed-lined canal at Wimbleford, the rowboats gather each evening and argue about who should steer. Tonight the little green dinghy volunteered first, so the others tied their ropes behind her and called out, cheerfully, which landings they could reach if asked. A heron offered to referee. When the dinghy grew tired, the oldest punt slid forward without fuss, and the whole string kept gliding past the bakery lights. Nobody bumped. *Everyone knew their place, and it changed whenever it needed to.*
