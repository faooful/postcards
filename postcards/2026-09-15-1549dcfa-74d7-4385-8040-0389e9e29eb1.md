The spring flood split our valley stream into two quarrelsome channels, and all summer the ferry-women of Confluence Bend rowed gossip between banks that might simply have whispered to each other. Then October arrived in soft boots. Without ceremony, the water found its old seam beneath the sand, and the halves slid together like hands remembering a handshake. The regatta committee, idle since June, stood up as one and began arguing about flags instead of geography. I watched from the grain-mill stairs and applauded alone. Some reunions need no announcement; they simply flow.
