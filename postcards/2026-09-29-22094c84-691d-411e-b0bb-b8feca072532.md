Every spring, Tarnhollow hires a clerk to answer the letters its old post office never sent. This year the clerk, a retired sea-cook named Beryn, unwrapped a bundle from a girl who asked whether the moon ever gets lonely. Beryn hiked to the return address, learned the girl now surveys wild rivers up north, and mailed her reply downstream to catch up with the season. Some letters, Beryn decided, simply need a longer road; the town agreed, and started planning next spring's backlog party.
