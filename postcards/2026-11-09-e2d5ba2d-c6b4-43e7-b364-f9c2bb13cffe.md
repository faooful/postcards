At the crossroads post office in the salt flats, the tortoises finally got sorting shelves. Every parcel used to land in one dune-high pile. Now each canyon has its own cubby, a hand-painted sign, and a pebble count chalked beside it. The ones stamped *urgent* glow faintly at dusk. A young tortoise named Bramble invented a rule: tap twice to send a letter on, once to bring it back. Nobody has lost a postcard since, though a lizard keeps hiding in the No Address bin, purely for the shade.
