In the river theatre at Brindle Shallows, the otter troupe spent all afternoon on one trick: the curtain had to glide across the stage without a single actor shuffling sideways. They greased the rails with clover honey, tried again, and again. By dusk the curtain swept past like a breeze over reeds, and the actors behind it stood perfectly still, holding their bows. The audience of herons applauded with slow, approving wingbeats.
