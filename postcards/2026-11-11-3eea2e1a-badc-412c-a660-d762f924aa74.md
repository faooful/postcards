In the valley of Ostrel, every village rang its own bell at its own idea of noon. Travelers arrived for lunch an hour early and left supper an hour late. So the clockmakers hiked up with one new rope pattern and rehung every tower in a single week. The bells still sound different, one gruff and one silvery, but each answers the same pull. Yesterday a goat herder set her watch by three villages at once and laughed out loud when they agreed.
