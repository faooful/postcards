In the hill town of Brindlecote, the stairwell glassblowers pour dusk down the steps each evening, one landing at a time. For years the landings stayed stubbornly grey while the sky above them glowed violet. This autumn an apprentice tried cutting each pane from the same long sheet of sunset, in order, so the color stepped down unbroken. Neighbors now pause on the third landing just to watch it carry on. Someone left a teacup there, *still warm*, as if to say thank you.
