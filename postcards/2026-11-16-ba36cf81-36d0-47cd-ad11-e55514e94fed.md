Every winter the salt galleries under Tamsin Ridge hold Echo Night. The mole-rat acrobats string lanterns from the crystal ceilings, and each cave takes its turn to sing back. The rule is old and simple: a cavern answers only the voice standing inside it. This year a nervous young tumbler shouted from the wrong ledge, and nothing came back but a polite, glittering hush. She climbed down, stood where she belonged, and called again. The whole hill hummed her name, and even the stalactites seemed pleased.
