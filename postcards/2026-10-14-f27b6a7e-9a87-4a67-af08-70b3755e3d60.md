Mira ran the last archive train on the delta line, the one that still stopped at stations nobody renamed. A courier arrived asking after a parcel sent years back, address half-remembered, platform uncertain.

Mira didn't promise the parcel. She promised the search. She walked every siding, matched manifests against dust, found three decoys and one false lead shaped exactly like hope. By dusk she had no parcel, but a clean map of where it wasn't, and a guess about which other line might still be running.

The courier left lighter anyway. A good search closes almost as well as a good find.
