At low tide the snails of Brimwhistle Cove decided their signboards had grown unruly: some leaned, some shouted, some hid behind kelp. So they set every board in a neat row along the warm rocks, each one saying exactly what it said before, only standing straighter. A crab tried to add a slogan and was politely handed a pebble instead. By evening the gulls could read the whole pool in one glide, and the snails, very slowly, felt rather proud.
