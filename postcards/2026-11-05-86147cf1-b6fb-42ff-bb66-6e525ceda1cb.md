Up on the salt flats, a young goat wandered off after a cloud shaped like a trombone and ended the day on a stranger's porch, wearing a tag nobody recognized. The village argued kindly over supper. Then the cobbler noticed her bell rang in the same key as the herd on the eastern ridge. At dusk they walked her up the switchbacks, and the whole flock stamped hello in the gravel. She slept warm, crowded close, finally counted with the others.
