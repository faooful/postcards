The otters who mind the river's locks had been juggling a lever for every gate, each with its own trick of rust and temper. Last week a quiet engineer from downstream walked the bank, hummed at the waterworks, and left behind one brass lever that lifts them all together. This morning the first barge slid through six gates without a single splash of complaint, and the otters spent the saved hour sunning on the weir, deeply pleased with the arithmetic of it.
