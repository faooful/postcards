At the Brindlemoor badger inn, every guest used to hang their hat on a peg they had carved themselves, so the hallway bristled like a hedgehog. This autumn the innkeeper built one long shelf of warm oak. There was grumbling, then a great deal of sniffing, then quiet. Now the hats sit in a tidy row, a felt cap beside a straw bonnet, and when the wind slams the front gate they all tip together and settle back. *Much better,* said the oldest badger, and promptly lost his.
