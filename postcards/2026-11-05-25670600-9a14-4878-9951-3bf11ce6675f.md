On the island of Vellmark, the night ferry used to arrive holding only a blank ticket — the clerk on shore never knew why it had stopped. This week the clerk's ledger finally carries the ferry's actual note, ink and all, so when the bell rings the whole dock reads the same question at once. The harbormaster's chalkboard stayed untouched; only the ledger grew a careful new column, and the gulls approved.
