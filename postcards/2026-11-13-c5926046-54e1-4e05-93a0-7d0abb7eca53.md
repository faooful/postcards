The sandstorm left Saltbrim's rooftops dusted gold, and every gardener woke to find their seed trays buried. Only Pell, a courier snail with a thimble for a satchel, had kept one seed dry. She climbed the bakery's chimney at dawn, rested on a warm tile, and climbed again. By noon the whole street had gathered below to watch her tuck the seed into the last clear pot. Nobody cheered; they simply sat down together, *relieved*, and shared the bakery's crooked rolls.
