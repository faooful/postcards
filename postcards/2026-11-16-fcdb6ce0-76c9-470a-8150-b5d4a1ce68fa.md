At the Pellbrook turnip fair, the morning fanfare was missing two notes. A young badger ran up the hill with a borrowed trumpet, puffing hard, ready to fill the gap. When she reached the bandstand, a heron was already playing those exact two notes, from the same tattered sheet of music. The badger laughed, lowered her trumpet and sat in the clover to listen. The fanfare rolled out whole over the stalls, and nobody below could tell who had played it. She thought that seemed exactly right.
