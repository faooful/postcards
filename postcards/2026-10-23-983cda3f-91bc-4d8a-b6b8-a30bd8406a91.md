Every winter in Pellford the snow owls borrow books from the hillside library, one each, and carry them off to their drafty steeples. This year the librarian woke to a gentle tapping: forty owls on the sill, each holding its card in its beak, every book marked at the exact page where it had stopped. One owl brought back a card for a book that had long since left the shelves, set it down politely, and flew off toward the next chapter of the storm.
