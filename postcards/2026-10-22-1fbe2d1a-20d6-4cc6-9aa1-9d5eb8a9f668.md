Up in the ice village of Brindlemoor, the children learned the glacier would hum if you tapped it in the right order. Nobody believed them until Old Fenwick, wrapped in four scarves, tapped it twice, then a third time slower. The whole valley answered in a low, contented chord. Snow hares sat up in the drifts. The baker forgot his ovens. By evening everyone was tapping, trying new orders, and every time the ice found a tune that fit.
