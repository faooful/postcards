On the morning the barley flooded, the stilt walkers of Pellow Reach discovered their baskets could carry more than anyone had ever asked of them. A small cousin tucked a sketch of a sleepy hedgehog in beside the bread, and nobody sent it back. By noon the whole line wobbled across the green water, parcels bulging happily, wading herons nodding like tiny inspectors. Nothing spilled. Everyone arrived a little taller than expected, and the hedgehog was pinned above the stove, where it stayed.
