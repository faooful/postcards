In the marsh village of Puddlewick, the otters grew tired of guessing which stones would hold them. So one drizzly afternoon they painted a small glowing symbol on each stone, then pinned a map to the reed hall wall showing every hop across the water. Old Grandmother Whisker read it twice and laughed out loud. That evening even the youngest pups crossed the whole river in the dark, skipping from stone to stone, and *nobody got wet at all*.
