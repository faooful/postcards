A goose checked into the porcelain hotel carrying nothing but a rolled carpet. She declined a room and asked for the roof.

By supper, every chimney wore a small red runner. The other geese landed politely, wiped their feet, and settled around the warm bricks. Below them, the concierge listened to the soft shuffle overhead and put away his complaints book.

For once, he said, the guests had brought their own idea of comfort. He sent up a pot of warm water.
