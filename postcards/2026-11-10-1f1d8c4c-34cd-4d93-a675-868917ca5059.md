In the dune town of Sallow Reach, someone hung a banner so long it trailed across three streets and tangled the camels. Nobody could read the end of it. So the town's tailors climbed the rooftops at noon, scissors glinting, and hemmed every banner to a tidy arm's length, folding the rest into neat little pockets for anyone curious. By evening the wind sang through the streets again, the camels walked straight, and the banners waved short and cheerful above the market.
