On the reef's quiet edge, a bicycle leans where the current left it, and a young coral has claimed the handlebars for itself. The divers who tend the nursery visit each week, not to move it, but to watch: petals of pink opening over the rusted spokes, a wrasse inspecting the bell. No one planned this garden. They simply kept showing up, and the sea did the rest. Sometimes the best tending is patience, and a place that lets small bright things arrive.
