In the valley of Pellow Rise, a red kite once drifted into the walled orchard and got tangled in the pear branches, where nobody could see it from the meadow. The children waved and waved. On Thursday a gust of polite wind came by, untied one knot with great ceremony, and carried the kite back over the wall. It landed in the clover beside its old friends, a little rumpled, very pleased, and the whole meadow cheered as if it had flown all the way to the sea.
