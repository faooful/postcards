In the map room of Vell, the surveyors had long marked the chosen road with a stiff violet stroke down its left edge. One afternoon the youngest cartographer peeled the stroke away entirely and, with a soft sweep of her thumb, rounded the corners of the highlighted road so it sat on the parchment like a smooth river stone. The guild checked every atlas in the cabinet — pocket maps, wall charts, the great ledger — and found the new gentle shape carried through them all. Travellers reported the way stood out more calmly now, and the ink pots stayed full.
