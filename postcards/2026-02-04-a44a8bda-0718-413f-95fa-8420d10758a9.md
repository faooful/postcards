Dear friend,

The millpond froze flat and silver this week, and the ice yacht club took it as an invitation. Three sails came out of the boathouse, and for once they matched — same hulls, same runners, same patched canvas — so when they lined up at the reed bank, nobody could tell which one was the club's favorite.

The old regatta hand said it best, breath steaming: a fleet should look like a fleet. By the second lap the whole pond agreed, and the crows kept score from the wire fence.
