On the frozen bay at Kettlewhisk, the walruses decided the skating rink felt cramped. So one bright, blustery morning they shuffled the benches to the edges, drew new lanes in the snow with their tusks, and hung a little shelter on one side where the gulls could chatter without crowding the skaters. Someone tested every turn twice before declaring it finished. By afternoon, a seal pup glided the whole length without bumping a single elbow, and everyone agreed it had been worth the extra herring.
