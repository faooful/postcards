By dusk the river steps had cooled enough to walk on, and the fireflies arrived the way they always do, unannounced and in numbers nobody could explain. Stallholders lit nothing; the beetles settled above the breads and the pepper jars and did the work themselves, drifting up the staircase from landing to landing until the whole market glowed like a sentence being read aloud. A child traded a button for a ripe persimmon. The river, keeping its own counsel, went on reflecting every bit of it.
