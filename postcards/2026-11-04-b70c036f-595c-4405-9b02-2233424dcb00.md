In the cave village of Underhollow, the glowworms had always shone as bright as they could, so nobody could find the stars painted on the ceiling. One evening the eldest glowworm suggested they try a gentler hum of light, brightening only when someone came near. The bats loved it. The children could finally trace the painted constellations, and whenever a traveller reached out, the nearest glowworm flared warm and helpful, as if it had been waiting all along.
