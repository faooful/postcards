On the river below Quillmarsh, a small night ferry collects echoes that wandered off from canyons. Each one boards with a ticket punched in a different shape, and the ferrywoman sorts them by mood into velvet-lined drawers. Tonight two echoes argued about which drawer was theirs, so she built a third with a brass label and a patient hinge. By dawn every echo had found its canyon again, humming the right answer back to whoever called. The river looked very pleased with itself.
