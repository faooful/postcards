The trams of Fernwick used to unfold their maps across every seat, so no one could see out the windows. This week the conductors folded them into tidy little ribbons pinned beside the doors, one painted symbol per stop. A heron, a bell, a spiral staircase. Passengers who want the whole map only need to tug the ribbon, and it blooms open again. Old Mrs. Pell rode the whole loop twice just to watch the hills go by.
