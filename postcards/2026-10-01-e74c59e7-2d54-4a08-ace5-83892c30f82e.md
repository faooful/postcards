By the time the frost lifted, Marta's bees had crossed the low stone wall and settled into Piet's empty hive as though they had read the ledger themselves. The two keepers met at the boundary, each holding half a jar, and laughed at how little arguing was left to do. Nettlefen called it swarm season; nobody gave a speech. The bees simply moved in, the honey darkened slightly, and the village agreed that autumn's harvest tasted like two summers at once.
