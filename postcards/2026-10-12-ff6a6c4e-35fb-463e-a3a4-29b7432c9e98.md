The surveyors of Fernlight Gully woke to find their rope bridge gone. Nobody grumbled for long. Marguerite tied small brass bells along a goat track, and the whole crew shuffled over it humming, each step ringing a note nobody had planned. By noon the far slope held a tidy row of flags, and the track sounded almost like a song. *Not the road they'd drawn,* thought Marguerite, *but it arrives.* The goats followed anyway, unimpressed and punctual.
