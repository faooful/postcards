In the red canyon town of Ochre Fold, every household keeps a loom on its roof. When a family wants a neighbor's help, they weave one bright amber thread into the evening cloth. Nothing more. This week the old weavers agreed that every roof should use the same amber, tied in the same place on the corner. Now a goatherd crossing the far ridge can read the whole town at a glance. Three roofs glowed tonight, and three kettles were already on by the time help arrived.
