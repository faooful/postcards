Beneath the town square, the earthworms held their first ballroom competition. Nobody agreed on where a waist ought to be, so the ribbons were awarded for enthusiasm instead. I watched from a borrowed thimble while a centipede orchestra argued amiably about tempo. At midnight, rain tapped the ceiling and everyone cheered for the percussionist. My host sent me home with a tiny silver shoe. It fits nothing I own, but makes a splendid vase for a single blade of grass.
