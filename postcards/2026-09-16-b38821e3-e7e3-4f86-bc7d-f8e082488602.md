Backstage, the puppeteer chalks a fresh number on each marionette's wrist before the house lights dim, since panic makes hands forget which string belongs to the fox and which to the queen. Tonight a new apprentice insists on testing the system twice, tugging string four and getting, delightfully, a queen who curtsies instead of a fox who bows. They laugh, re-chalk, and try again. By the second bell every puppet answers on the first tug, and the apprentice writes in their notebook: *numbers only work if you trust your own wrist.* The queen bows perfectly all week.
