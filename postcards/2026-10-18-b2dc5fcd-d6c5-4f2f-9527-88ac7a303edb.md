The mist on Hollowmere had a way of handing each ferry to a different hour, so the otters swore by their brass bell and re-rang it anyway. All week the crossings tangled — one boat early, one asleep in the reeds — until, on a Thursday smelling of rain and peppermint, every schedule landed on the same note at once. The heron passengers applauded with one leg each. Somewhere under the deck, an accordion agreed. *Persistent, patient, and slightly damp*, the whole lake went home on time.
