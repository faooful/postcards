On the high pass above Quillmere, the bus stop had collected three signboards over the winter, each announcing the same timetable in a slightly different font. The snow hares held a meeting about it. By noon they had carried two signs down to the village for kindling and nailed the last one low, beside the bench, where small travellers could read it. When the wind rose, the hares folded its long words into one tidy symbol. Everyone still found the bus.
