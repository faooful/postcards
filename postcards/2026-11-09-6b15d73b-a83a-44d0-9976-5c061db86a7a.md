In the valley of slow bells, the snails gather each dawn on a mossy wall to sing. One morning the smallest snail kept arriving a whisker late, always one leaf to the right of everyone else. Nobody scolded her. Instead, the old snail with the striped shell noticed an empty acorn cap sitting where nobody stood, quietly taking up room. They rolled it into the brook. The next morning she sang shoulder to shoulder with the others, and the whole wall hummed in a perfectly even line.
