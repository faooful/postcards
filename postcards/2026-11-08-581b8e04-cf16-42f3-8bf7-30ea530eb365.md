The fen surveyors had argued for a season about one crooked line on the county chart, so they climbed the scaffolding at first light and redrew the whole meridian as a single clean stroke, mist rising off the water like a fresh erasure. By noon an unnamed islet appeared where the old tangle used to be, and the youngest apprentice got to name it. They chose "Finally." The guild sealed the chart, hung it in the north window, and put the kettle on.
