Down at the harbor there was a lamppost that never quite caught when you brushed past it, while its neighbors glowed right away. Today someone finally rewired it to match the others — same warm flicker, same easy patience. A gull landed on the crossbar, approved with a single nod, and the whole row along the dock lit up together, steady and unbothered by the wind coming in off the water.
