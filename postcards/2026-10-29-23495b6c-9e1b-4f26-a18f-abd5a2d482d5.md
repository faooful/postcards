In the attic town of Wickerhollow, the moths had worn their grandest cloaks to the autumn ball: tassels, ribbons, three pockets each. Nobody could fit through the chimney door. So the eldest moth, very gravely, snipped away everything but one small silver button. The rest were folded into a hatbox by the stairs, for anyone curious to lift the lid. That night they slipped through the door in a single quiet line, and the dancing went on until the candle stubs gave up.
