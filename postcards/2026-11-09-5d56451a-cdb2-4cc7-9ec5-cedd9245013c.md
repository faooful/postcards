In the valley this autumn, every farm stitched its own patch of the great quilt, and no two hems agreed. The goat farm left a wide border, the mill a narrow one, the beekeepers none at all. So the quilters walked the fences with one thimble each, pinning every edge to the same quiet line. By evening the whole hillside lay flat and even, and the wind, for once, found nothing loose to tug.
