In the valley town of Quillinby, the river had two names: the long one the grandmothers used and the short one the children shouted. Travelers asking for either got shrugs, so the mapmakers painted both on every signpost, the long name tall and the short one tucked beneath. By Thursday a lost cheesemonger found the ferry on her first try and celebrated by rolling a wheel of something blue down the hill. Nobody stopped it. The river took it gracefully.
