Beneath the abandoned bandstand, a mushroom was selling tickets to a concert nobody could hear. I bought the last seat. The audience sat quite still while a mole in white gloves conducted the roots overhead.

Halfway through, my boots began tapping without me. A woman beside me nodded as though this settled an old argument.

After the applause, the mushroom returned my ticket. It had sprouted a tiny velvet curtain. I keep it folded in my pocket for quiet evenings.
