On Ferrow Lane the umbrellas used to shout. Each one was painted electric blue, so whenever it rained the whole street looked alarmed. This autumn a quiet tinsmith offered the umbrella stand a soft gray awning, tucked just above its doorway like a cat's ear. Nobody noticed at first, which was exactly the point. Now the umbrellas hum instead of shout, and pigeons nap beneath the awning, and the rain arrives *politely*, as though it had knocked first.
