In Halden's Fold, the echoes kept a small museum of everything the valley ever said. One October they went quiet, and the town woke to footsteps that made no sound and doors that closed politely into nothing. Meret, who ran the lost-and-found, hiked up through the fog with a tin of warm stones. She found the echoes wintering in a crease of the cliff, shy as sparrows, and promised they could stay as long as they liked. By dusk they followed her home anyway, rehearsing, one by one, everything worth saying twice.
