At the Mossbridge county fair, the tortoises who finished the long race were once given ribbons pinned crookedly to their shells. Visitors kept mistaking them for stray thread and tried to tug them loose. So this year the judges stitched every ribbon into a small round rosette, bright as marigolds, and the tortoises paraded past the hay bales looking *wonderfully* official. Children pointed. One tortoise, very pleased, insisted on doing a second lap just to be admired.
