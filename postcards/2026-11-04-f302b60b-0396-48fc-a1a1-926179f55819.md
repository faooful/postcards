In the village of Lower Thimble, the hedgehogs woke to find a crosswalk stripe leading nowhere, painted straight into the hedge. Nobody remembered painting it. They held a small meeting under a dandelion, voted with their noses, and scrubbed the stray stripe away by moonlight. The snails, who had been waiting politely at that stripe for a week, finally crossed somewhere sensible. By dawn the road read cleanly again, and a passing beetle declared it the tidiest street in the county.
