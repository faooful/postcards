At the river library, the otters decided the bell for closing time belonged by the front desk, not buried behind the atlases. So they carried it over, one paw each, and hung the calendar beside it where everyone could reach. Nobody wrote a single new word. They only moved the shelves until the light fell evenly across every table. By evening a heron came in, found exactly what she wanted on the first try, and stayed to read until the frogs began to sing.
