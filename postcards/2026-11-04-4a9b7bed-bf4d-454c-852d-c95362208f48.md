High on the Col de Virelle, the little switchboard hut woke to a sky gone crooked. The storm had looped every wire into long, impatient spirals, and calls were spilling clean past the hut's edge into the ravine. The Virelle sisters — Margot with the sharp pencil, Anouk with the endless patience — gave each tangled line room to fold back on itself, coil by coil, until every voice curled neatly inside the switchboard again. By dusk the ravine held only weather, and the mountain's conversations stayed exactly where they belonged.
