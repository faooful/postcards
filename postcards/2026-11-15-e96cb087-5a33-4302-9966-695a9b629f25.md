The otters of Ferngate used to ring every doorbell in the valley whenever a single letter arrived. This season they tried something new: they read the envelope first, and knocked only where it was meant to go. Suddenly the hedgehogs slept through the afternoon, the voles finished their crossword, and the old badger on the hill said the valley sounded *roomy*. The otters kept their whistles, though. Some mornings bring a whole sack at once, so they wait one breath, then deliver it all together.
