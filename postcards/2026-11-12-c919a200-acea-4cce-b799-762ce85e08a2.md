The hill town used to nail a separate brass plate to every lamppost: one for the bakery stop, one for the baths, one for the cliff lift. Visitors counted plates instead of scenery. Yesterday the transit guild unscrewed the lot and hung a single wooden flap by the fountain, engraved "More". Lift the flap and every stop in the valley is listed in tidy ink, no pins, no favorites, no debates. The lampposts stand lighter now, and the fountain reports the flap has already been lifted forty times, mostly out of delight.
