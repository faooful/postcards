Dear you,

Quail Junction has one turnaround loop, four bus companies, and a dispatcher named Marisol who keeps the master board in chalk. This week two depots sent her two rival timetables, each accurate on its own and each sure the other was a rumor. Drivers had been politely arguing over the same 4:15 for days.

Marisol laid both timetables side by side, read every line aloud, and kept whichever entry had actually seen the road. Where both sides claimed the 4:15, she checked the loop itself, not the paper. By dusk the board was one timetable again, no crossed-out buses and no orphaned slots, and she rewarded herself with the good blue chalk she saves for schedules that survive contact with reality.
