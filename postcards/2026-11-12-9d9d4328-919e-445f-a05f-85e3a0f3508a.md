A warm wind climbed the mountain last night and unhitched winter from the observatory railings. Inside, the cook declared it soup weather and set the great copper pot between two telescopes. Astronomers who had squinted at cold stars for weeks unclenched their shoulders and argued happily about constellations nobody could see through the fog of steam. By morning the frost had retreated to the very summit, where it sat like a polite guest waiting to be invited back down. The pot, scraped clean, was already humming for tomorrow.
