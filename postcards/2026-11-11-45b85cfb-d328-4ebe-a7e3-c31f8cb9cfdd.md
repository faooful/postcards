In the village of Ostrel Hollow, the old well had always been too deep to see the bottom of. This autumn a young mole named Perrin started carving steps into its walls, one each evening, by the light of glowworms who had volunteered for the job. Neighbors leaned over the rim to watch. On the ninth night Perrin reached the water and found a small door, painted green, that nobody had known about. Behind it: a reading room, perfectly dry, waiting.
