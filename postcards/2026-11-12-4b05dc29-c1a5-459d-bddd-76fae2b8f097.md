In Vellum Square the town crier had a habit of reading proclamations clear to the end, no matter how long the scroll, and the plaza would simply wait, hats in hand, while a whole afternoon's news unrolled. This morning the council tried something new: one line of the scroll at the gate, the full text still rolled and ready for anyone who leaned in to read. The square emptied by lunch, and the crier discovered she had time for coffee.
