Up at the Thimbleback ski hut the snow hares hold their supper on one long bench. For years the latecomers would plop down right on top of whoever was already eating, and everyone ended up with mittens in their soup. This winter the eldest hare tried something new: when a guest knocks, the whole row shuffles over one seat, and a fresh place appears at the end. Nobody gets squashed. The cocoa stays warm, and the window stays clear for watching the snow.
