On the cape at Ferrow's End, the lighthouse keeper noticed something odd: long after the ships had passed, her lamp kept glowing anyway. It had simply grown fond of staying lit, like a guest who forgets the party ended. She climbed the stairs, patted the brass, and explained kindly that rest was part of shining too. The lamp blinked, understood, and dimmed to a soft ember just as the fog rolled in like a curious cat. Everything on the cape felt lighter afterward, even the gulls.
