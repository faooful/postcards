Every spring the highland flock trotted home along the old cart track, even after the village laid a bright new road through the heather. So the sheepdogs held a meeting under a dripping rowan tree. *Whenever the bells ring a new tune*, the eldest declared, *we start from the crossroads.* The next morning the bells rang something unfamiliar, and the dogs fanned out, nudging every woolly wanderer toward fresh gravel. By noon the whole flock was grazing happily beside the new fence, wondering why they had ever liked mud.
