At the underground bathhouse, a small thunderstorm arrived wrapped in a towel. The attendant offered it the warm pool and politely requested indoor rumbling. Soon the other bathers were leaning back against the tiles, enjoying a particularly convincing massage of distant thunder. Nobody minded the occasional drizzle. When the storm finally left, pink and pleasantly exhausted, it forgot its slippers. The attendant set them beside the door, where they continued to squeak softly whenever anyone mentioned rain.
