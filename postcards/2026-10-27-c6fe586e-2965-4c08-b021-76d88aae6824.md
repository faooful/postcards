In the village of Brindle Wick, the hat library had always kept its bonnets in three crooked wardrobes, each with its own creaky hinge. One drizzly afternoon a family of ferrets moved everything onto a single long rail that slid gently along the wall. Now a borrowed sun hat glides out when asked and tucks itself away again without a fuss. The postmistress tried on a feathered cap twice, just to watch the rail hum. Everyone agreed the shop felt roomier, though nothing had actually left.
