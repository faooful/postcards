At low tide the tortoises of Brindlecove open their market among the rock pools, and this morning they decided every stall should face the same way. Nobody changed what was for sale: the same periwinkle jam, the same knotted kelp scarves. They only nudged the awnings into neat rows and drew chalk lines between the aisles. By noon the crabs could find the barnacle seller without asking twice, and the gulls, for once, landed in a tidy queue.
