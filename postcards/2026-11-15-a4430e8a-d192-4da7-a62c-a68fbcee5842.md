In Perrin Hollow, the last summer glacier shrank to a blue pebble, and the ice-masons knocked off work for good. They met one final time in the syrup-scented mill, signed the survey in violet ink, and pinned it where the frost used to argue. Marta, who had measured the melt for forty-one years, danced badly and wonderfully on a table. Someone rang the mill bell until the sparrows complained. The pebble went to the museum, labeled simply: *finished*.
