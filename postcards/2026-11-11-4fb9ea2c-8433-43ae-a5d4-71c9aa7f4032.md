For three days the rain drifted above Quill Valley, unable to decide which slope to settle on. Villagers hauled buckets onto roofs, not to catch it, but to make their shingles sound interesting. By evening the storm picked the barn with the tin gutter, and everyone agreed it had chosen well. The next morning the well was higher, the frogs were louder, and the mail arrived dry for once.
