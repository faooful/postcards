Underneath the clover meadow, the badgers decided their burrow had too many doors and not enough hallway. So they spent a rainy week digging one long corridor, lining it with little signposts carved from acorn caps. Each room got a tidy alcove; the pantry learned to fold itself small when guests arrived. Visitors now wander in, spot the glowing moss lamps marking where they are, and never once end up in the sock cupboard by mistake. The badgers are thrilled, and slightly muddy.
