This morning the fog market at Tessaly Cove decided its stalls were too far apart. The glassblowers agreed. By noon they had nudged every booth into one long, bright row, so you could walk from the bubble-sellers to the ribbon-weavers without losing your hat to the wind. A small goat inspected each booth, nodding when the awnings lined up. The fog seemed pleased too: it settled lower, politely, as if someone had finally tidied the room.
