In Millbrask the canal barges had always moored however they pleased: one sideways, one nose-first, one tied to a willow out of stubbornness. This autumn the ferrywoman painted a single white stripe along the quay, and by Thursday every barge had drifted over to line up against it. The baker's barge grumbled about losing its favorite puddle of shade, but it found another one. Now the ducks can walk the whole length of the canal without once swimming, and they seem very pleased about it.
