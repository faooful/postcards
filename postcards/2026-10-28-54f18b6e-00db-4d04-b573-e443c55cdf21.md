In the dune market of Sorrel Wash, three beetle merchants used to shout over each other about which road led to water. Travelers mostly stood there blinking. This morning the eldest beetle painted one tidy signpost listing every road, the nearest at the top in cheerful blue, and the shouting just stopped. A camel read the sign, nodded, and ambled off with no fuss at all. The beetles shared a single date in the shade and agreed the quiet sounded *wonderful*.
