At the top of the Old Copper Tram line, the conductor found the morning crowd jumbled together — hikers, skiers, and one confused accordionist — all pressed at the same switch. So she painted two new signs on the platform, counted each crowd twice, and lit a small lamp over each carriage showing its number, so no one ever boarded for the wrong valley again. The accordionist, it turned out, was welcome in both. The mountain approved, and the switch hummed in two clear voices all winter.
