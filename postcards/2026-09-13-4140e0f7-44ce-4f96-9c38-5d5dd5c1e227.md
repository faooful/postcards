The giant arrived at the pottery stall with a thimble between two fingers. Could we mend the handle? It was his favourite teacup, he explained, although drinking from it took most of the afternoon.

The potter worked beneath a magnifying glass while he waited on the hillside. His knees made two comfortable benches for the children.

When the handle was sound, he paid with a pebble of amber. There was a whole orange sunset caught inside it, still warm.
