Off the cold coast of wherever the maps run out, an old library rests on the seafloor, its shelves lit by patient fish. Every tide, eight armfuls of curiosity slide the returned volumes home — poetry by the coral, atlases where the light falls longest. A drifting visitor once asked why bother, so far from any reader. A small octopus pressed the last book flush into its slot, and the whole aisle seemed to sigh. *Some things sit straighter*, it seemed to say, *just because someone cared.*
