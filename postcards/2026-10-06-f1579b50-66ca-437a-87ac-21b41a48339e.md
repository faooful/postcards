Dear friend,

In the loom shed above the harbor steps, two apprentice weavers spent the morning untangling a warp that had been threaded from opposite ends. Every few inches the threads crossed and argued; every few inches they nodded, chose the truer strand, and tied a knot small enough to hide in the pattern. By dusk the cloth hung whole, and nobody could point to a single knot. The shed cat approves. Wish you were here with the tea.
