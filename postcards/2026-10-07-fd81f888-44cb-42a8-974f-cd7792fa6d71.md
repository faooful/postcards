Deep under the cliffs, the cavern glassmakers had two lamps that looked identical, and travelers kept taking the wrong tunnel. Marguerite, the youngest, shaped a new lamp with a tiny forked handle, so a glance told you which passage led where. By evening the tunnels hummed with *confident* footsteps. Nobody got lost, the soup stayed hot, and an old goat who had wandered the wrong way for years finally arrived at supper exactly on time.
