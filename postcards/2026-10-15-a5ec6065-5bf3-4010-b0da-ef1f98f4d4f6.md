Down in the canyon, the otters decided their rope bridge had grown crooked over the summer. Nobody had fallen, but the planks wandered like a sentence that forgot its point. So they spent one windy afternoon untying every knot and tying it again in the same order, left to right, plank by plank. When they finished, a heron walked across without looking down once. The otters pretended not to notice, then cheered loudly from the rocks below.
