On the spiral stairs of Hollowmere, a brass bell used to ring whenever anyone passed, even when there was nothing in the cellar to fetch. The beekeepers grew weary of climbing toward an empty jar. One drowsy afternoon they simply dimmed the bell and hung a small, honest sign beneath it. Now the stairs are hushed when the cellar is bare, and the bell glows again when honey arrives. Travelers say the staircase finally feels like it means what it says, and the bees hum a little louder, pleased.
