On Pumice Ridge the wind arrives folded, like an accordion, and the goats have learned to wait for it to open. This afternoon it came in three pleats. The first carried a lost mitten. The second carried a smell of toasted barley from somewhere far below. The third carried nothing at all, just a long, satisfied sigh that rolled over the scree and settled the dust. The goats lay down. A marmot came out to read the clouds, decided they said *nothing urgent*, and went back inside.
