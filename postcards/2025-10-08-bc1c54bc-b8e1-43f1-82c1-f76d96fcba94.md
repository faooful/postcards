Down at the night market, the potters grew tired of their fixed stall rows and decided everything should move freely. They dragged their shelves wherever the evening breeze suggested, grouped the glazed bowls into little plazas, and even kept a tiny map on a string so no one lost a kiln after dark. By midnight the whole market breathed like a single living sketch — and the potters, delighted, stayed up late just rearranging it.
