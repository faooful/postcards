Every evening the harbor master of Brindle Cove calls the names of boats returning home, and the cliff lighthouse blinks once for each. Tonight the newest skiff, painted mustard yellow, slid in unannounced. The lighthouse stayed dark, sulking. A gull noticed the harbor master's list had been folded right through one name, splitting it in two. Smoothed flat, the page read clearly again, and the lamp gave the little skiff a long, warm sweep across the water.
