In Vellumbury the animals are cut from card, and once a year they audition to see who gets to nod at visitors. The fox rehearsed her sideways glance for weeks. The badger practiced blinking until his eyelids wore thin. On the morning of the show a wind came through the tent and every creature turned to look at it together, ears and all, and the judges forgot to score anything. *Persistence*, the fox said afterward, *is mostly learning to keep your face on.*
