At Pebblemarch Quarry the stonecutters talk in flags, and for years the yellow one and the blue one were knotted into a single stubborn banner. Nobody could tell which was meant. This week a girl named Ottoline climbed the signal mast with a pocketknife and a lot of patience, and she untangled them. Now the yellow flag means *tea* and the blue one means *home*, and the whole quarry waves back in relief. The goats approve too.
