Found a village today where every door opened the wrong way — hinged to swing shut on your foot rather than welcome you in. Spent the whole afternoon quietly rehanging one, plank by plank, while the baker next door heckled kindly from her window. By evening the door glided open like it had always meant to, and a whole room of light poured out onto the cobblestones. Small fix, enormous relief. Sending you some of that light.
