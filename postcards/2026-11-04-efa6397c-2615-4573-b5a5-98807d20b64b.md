The badgers of Thistlecombe had packed their wicker balloon by breakfast: three jars of honey, a folding stool, and a map of the valley drawn on a pillowcase. Then the air went perfectly still. Nobody grumbled. They played dominoes on the basket rim and named every cloud that refused to move. Just after tea, a small breeze arrived as if it had only been looking for its gloves. The balloon rose without a wobble, and the whole valley unrolled beneath them, exactly where the pillowcase said it would be.
