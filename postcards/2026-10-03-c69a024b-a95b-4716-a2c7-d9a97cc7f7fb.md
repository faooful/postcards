At the municipal swimming bath in Pell, a moth arrived wearing an astonishingly small bathing cap. The attendant offered a thimble of warm water and politely turned away. Soon the whole ceiling was crowded with swimmers awaiting their turn. Nobody splashed. Nobody asked about the deep end. By closing time, the attendant had reserved Wednesdays for wings and installed a diving board made from a bent grass stem. The first customer returned to complain that the towels were much too heavy.
