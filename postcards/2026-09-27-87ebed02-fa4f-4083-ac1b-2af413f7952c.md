Twice a day the mountain trains pull into Hollowpine Junction carrying weather instead of freight — snow packed from the northern pass, warm wind rolled up from the salt flats. Marisol, who keeps the platform chalkboard, logs each arrival like cargo: "Tuesday, rain-smell from the west by four." This week an early frost rode in unannounced, silvering the platform edges while everyone was still planning for autumn. She wrote it up in blue chalk, and the whole station wandered out to watch winter practice its first careful lines. *Curiosity travels well.*
