On the frozen canal behind the bakery, the otters opened a rink where every skater could ask one question per lap. A grandmother wanted to know if the ice would hold a piano. A boy asked whether spins counted as laps. The otters answered by sliding past and tapping a little bell: once for yes, twice for maybe tomorrow. By sunset nobody was waiting in line anymore, and the piano played a slow waltz right in the middle of the ice.
