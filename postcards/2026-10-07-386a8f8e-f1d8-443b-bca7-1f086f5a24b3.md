Up at the alpine relay hut, the hares took over the sorting after the avalanche shuffled every parcel into one heap. They worked by whisker-light, stacking letters into tidy little towers, each topped with a pebble so the wind would mind its manners. By dawn the mule driver found rows so neat he forgot his grumbling and simply stood there, steam rising from his mug, admiring how calm a mountain looks when everything finally knows where it belongs.
