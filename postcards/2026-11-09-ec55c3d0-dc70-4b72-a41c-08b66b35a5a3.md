Above the salt flats of Wendle, the balloon pilots never cast off on a shout alone. Each passenger picks a direction by tugging a coloured ribbon, and the basket hums for three slow breaths before rising. Last Tuesday a goat tugged the purple ribbon by accident. Everyone laughed, the pilot untied it, and the balloon stayed put while the goat chewed thoughtfully on a map. By dusk the sky was full of baskets drifting exactly where people meant them to go.
