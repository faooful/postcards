The chalk miners of Hollow Verene go home before dark; they say the canyon repeats whatever it hears, twice, in a softer voice. Last night a traveler camped at the rim and hummed a wandering tune while her kettle heated. The canyon did not repeat it. It hummed along — harmonizing under its breath, patient and slightly off-key. By midnight half the town had climbed up to listen, kettles forgotten, boots pale with chalk dust. Nobody spoke. The tune ended, the echo finished its part last, and everyone descended grinning, certain the canyon had waited years for a duet.
