At Otterbend, the north river and the south river had argued for a week about which of them should run through the old mill race. The otters grew tired of swimming in circles. So they built a small weir of reeds and borrowed pebbles that let each river keep its own song and still share a channel. The first morning the water ran clear, a heron stood in the shallows looking almost impressed. *Persistence*, the otters decided, tastes faintly of mint.
