In the village of Crumbrook, the owls volunteered to check the autumn turnip ledger before the harvest fair. They read every column twice by lantern, hooting softly at a smudge here, a crooked sum there. Nothing was ruined, they announced, only two turnips had been filed under the wrong cart. The farmers laughed, moved the turnips, and served everyone warm cider. The owls went home at dawn, feathers ruffled, very pleased with themselves.
