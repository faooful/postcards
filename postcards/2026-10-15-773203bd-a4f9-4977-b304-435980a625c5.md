This morning the village cloud burst indoors again, so we caught the rain in jars. Thunder went on the top shelf, drizzle in the middle, and the soft mist that smells of pine at the bottom, where the children can reach it. A heron came by to borrow a puddle and was delighted to find it labelled. By noon the hall was dry and you could find anything you wanted. One jar is still empty. We're saving it for whatever weather comes next.
