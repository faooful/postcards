Five weavers arrived at a canyon where the wind hummed in two keys at once. Each carried a spool of a different colour and a stubborn hope. They launched their threads separately, and by afternoon the gap was strung with bright, wobbling bridges. One thread frayed, so its weaver simply knotted it and waved. By dusk a goat had crossed all five, unimpressed, and the canyon gently hummed in a single, contented key.
