The canyon market at Tamarind Gap sells the same blue salt twice: once from the high stalls, once from the low. Old Brisa finally noticed after thirty winters of dizzy arithmetic. She did not scold anyone. She hung a small brass bell on the high stalls and moved each duplicate crate there, one at a time, while the low stalls fell politely quiet. By dusk every crate lived in exactly one place, and a chalk tally by the gate whispered how many had travelled upward. The wind, relieved, stopped rearranging the awnings. *Everything counted, nothing lost.*
