Under the toadstool awnings of Dampwick Market, three snails set out at dawn to learn which stall sold what. They expected to chart every corner themselves. Instead they found each vendor had already chalked a tidy sign: spores here, puddles there, a small bell for questions. So the snails simply strung the signs together into one silver trail. By the evening drizzle, any beetle could follow it from the gate to the very last stall without once getting lost.
