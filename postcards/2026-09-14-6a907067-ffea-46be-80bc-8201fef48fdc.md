Mira had drawn the harbor map a hundred times, but tonight the ink wanted a new detail: a second color for the boats already moored, so they'd match the ones still sailing in. She held the lamp close, comparing shade against shade until both fleets read as one calm village. A gull outside kept score, unimpressed, but Mira noticed the small rightness of it anyway — nothing overlapping, nothing crowded off the page, just a harbor finally telling the truth about itself in a single, matching light.
