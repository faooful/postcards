Mira ran the relay post where two mountain lines met: one climbing from the valley, one dropping from the ridge. Most days a single lamp-flash sufficed. That morning both lines blinked at once, each insisting the other had the time wrong. She copied both messages into her logbook, found the three words they actually disagreed on, and sent back one corrected line to each post. By noon the valley confirmed. By two, the ridge did too. Neither ever knew the other had been talking past it; they only knew the lamp had gone quiet, then green.
