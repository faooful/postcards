Up on the windward ridge the glassblowers work only when the gusts agree. Each morning they line their newest bottles along a shelf, oldest at the far end, and the wind picks one to sing through. Travelers stop to listen, then lean closer to read the little paper tags tied at each neck: where the sand came from, which fire shaped it. Today a goat knocked the whole row into a new order, and somehow it sounded *better*. Nobody has put them back.
