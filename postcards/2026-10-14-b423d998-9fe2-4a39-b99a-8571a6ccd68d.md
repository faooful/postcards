In the hill village of Pell, every rooftop weathervane had stopped agreeing on which way the wind blew. The cartwrights couldn't find the market. So a small tortoise named Odile climbed the bell tower with a satchel of fresh maps, retied the oldest vane with ribbon, and taught the stubborn copper rooster a new direction. By supper, every arrow pointed down the orchard road, and the carts rolled out, rattling with pears and quiet relief.
