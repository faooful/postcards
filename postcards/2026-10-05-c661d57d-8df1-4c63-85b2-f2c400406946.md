At Quillmere Junction the signboard pointed east, but the tortoises had already packed up and gone west, leaving only a chalk arrow and a polite note on the bench. I followed it through three villages of sleepy goats and one very opinionated weathervane. When I finally found them, they had set up a whole new station, with brass bells and a tea cart, and they were delighted someone had read the note.
