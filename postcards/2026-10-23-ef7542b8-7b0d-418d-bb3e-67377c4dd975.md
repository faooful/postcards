On the night the tram rails learned to hum, Ivo the conductor stopped watching his watch. The whole line ran two minutes slow, and by some quiet arithmetic the town arrived early to everything it dreaded: the dentist, the difficult letter, the farewell. Riders traded seats for the pleasure of the lag, and the last car emptied only when the streetlamps blinked twice. Ivo kept the doors open an extra breath. Nobody minded. Slow, it turned out, was a place you could visit.
