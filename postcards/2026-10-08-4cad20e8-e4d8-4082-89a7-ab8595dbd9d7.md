The hedge maze at Pommel Hollow had been replanted overnight, and the old map insisted a gate stood where only plum trees now leaned. Odalie, who surveyed mazes for a living, stood puzzled until a passing goose waddled through the "wall" and quacked in triumph. So she crossed out the gate, drew in the goose's path, and measured the new spacing between the rows with a ribbon. By afternoon the map matched the orchard again, and the plums, pleased, dropped one gently into her pocket.
