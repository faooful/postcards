This year the bakers rehung their wire between two new bell towers, higher and wider than the old ones. Nobody was sure the *crown loaf* would survive the move. That's the braided one with a little copper bird baked into its crust. So the head baker carried it across herself, one slow step at a time, while the crowd hummed to keep her steady. Halfway over, the bird caught the wind and started to sing. She reached the far tower with flour on her boots and the loaf still warm.
