At the frozen pond behind the mill, every skater wears a little sewn badge so the judges can tell the twirlers from the racers. All winter one shy skater glided about with a bare sleeve, and nobody could remember her name. This morning a heron in a woolly scarf flew in with a needle and a bright patch. Now she spins past the reeds, sleeve flashing, and the whole crowd waves as if they had always known her.
