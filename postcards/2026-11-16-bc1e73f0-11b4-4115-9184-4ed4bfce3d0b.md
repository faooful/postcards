In the hill village of Pellmoss, the beekeepers wear hats stacked two brims high, one for sun and one, nobody remembers why, for more sun. This spring the bees complained, politely, by humming in a lower key. So the eldest beekeeper took her shears and snipped the upper brim clean away. Nothing fell down. The bees hummed brighter and the hives seemed closer to the sky. That evening, everyone walked home lighter, a little surprised they had carried the extra brim for so long.
