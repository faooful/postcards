On Bramblewick's crooked roof, the weathervanes agreed to share the watch. The copper rooster minds the west wind, the tin fox minds the east, and a small brass snail rings a bell only when a storm truly arrives. Neighbors no longer climb ladders at dawn to check every chimney. They glance up, see who is watching what, and go back to their porridge. Tonight a gust knocked once, the snail rang, and the fox turned quietly to meet it.
