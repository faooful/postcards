On the hillside tram at Vellamere, the conductor has only three bells and refuses to want a fourth. One bell means *look left*, where the valley spreads out like a letter still being written. Two bells mean *look ahead*, at the next stop and whoever is waving. Three bells mean *look right*, at the long wall of windows where the whole town shows itself at once. Passengers who try to do all three at once get gently seated. Those who wait for the bells arrive calm, having seen everything in order.
