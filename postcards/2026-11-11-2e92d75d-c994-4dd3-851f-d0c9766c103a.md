Out on the salt flats, the evening caravan arrived with more lanterns than the road was wide. Nobody panicked. The camels tucked their bells in, the tinkers folded their awnings to pocket size, and a small girl in a striped scarf walked ahead, measuring the dark with her arms outstretched. Only when the road truly narrowed did anyone step onto the second path. By moonrise *(fine, starlight)* every lantern still glowed, none hidden, all in a tidy, generous line.
