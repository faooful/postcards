At dawn the glassblowers of Orrin Flats tied their kites to fence posts and waited. The wind arrived late, as it always did, smelling faintly of rain that never fell. One small girl named Pim checked every knot twice, then once more, because *patience* felt like the whole job. When the first kite finally lifted, the others followed in a rustling line, and the whole flat sky hummed like a kettle. Nobody cheered. They simply sat down in the warm dust, relieved, and passed around a single, slightly lopsided loaf of bread.
