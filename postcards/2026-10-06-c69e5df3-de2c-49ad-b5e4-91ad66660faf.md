Downstream of Quarry Hollow, two rivers had quarreled for a season, each insisting the delta belonged to its own current. The ferrywoman of the middle sandbar simply waited, poles crossed, until the waters tired of arguing. One morning the channels braided together without ceremony, and the herons could not remember which bank had been whose. She poled across the new shallows and pronounced it a fine map at last, though nobody had asked her.
