The first snow fell on Tumbril Yard before the clocks were ready for it. The crane drivers took it personally. By noon every flatbed wore a white cap, and someone had built a snowman on the weighbridge, so the scale proudly claimed he weighed as much as a small barge. The night foreman hung lamps along the fence for no reason anyone could name. At dusk the whole yard glowed like a toy left out on purpose, and nobody went home on time.
