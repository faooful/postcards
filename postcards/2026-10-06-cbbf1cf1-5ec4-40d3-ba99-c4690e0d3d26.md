Out on the salt flats, the herons have formed a census committee for clouds. Each morning they line up on one leg and tally whatever drifts past: fat ones, thin ones, a suspicious cloud shaped like a wheelbarrow. Nobody asked them to. Yesterday a heron noticed that half the clouds were the same cloud, circling back to be counted again. The committee had a long, dignified argument about it, then decided to count it once and go wading instead.
