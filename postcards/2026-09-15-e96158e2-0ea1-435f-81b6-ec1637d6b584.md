The floating night market only opens when the fog forgets to leave. I paid two buttons to enter and found vendors auctioning rumors of tomorrow's weather — a bidder swore by hail, another by a warm wind smelling of oranges, a third by nothing at all. I bought all three and pinned them above my bunk. In the morning it did something none of them predicted, which felt, somehow, like the market winking. I'm keeping the buttons for next week.
