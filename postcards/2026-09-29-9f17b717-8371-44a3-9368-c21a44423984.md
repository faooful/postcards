Dear you — the season at the Saltmarrow playhouse ran sideways: the second act met the town in June, the finale arrived in July, and for weeks nobody could say what the story was about. Then one cool October evening, Nell the stage manager walked the company through it from the very first line to the last, in order, at last. The audience rose slowly at the curtain, the way people do when something finally makes sense. Wish you had been in the third row.
