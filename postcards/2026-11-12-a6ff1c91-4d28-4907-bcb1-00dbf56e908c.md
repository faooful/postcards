The last cable car of the season groans up to Halden Ridge with its winter sacks lashed tight, and Ilse, the attendant, marks each one off her chalkboard with a satisfied little circle. Frost feathers the windows; the valley below looks like a quilt someone shook out and forgot to smooth. At the top, the count is right — every sack, every strap, every knot — and Ilse tips her cap to the mountainside as the cable hums itself quiet. Snow starts as she descends, soft as applause. She hums the whole way down.
