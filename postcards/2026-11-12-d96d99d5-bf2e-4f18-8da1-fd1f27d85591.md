In the hill town of Quillmarsh, letters travel by snail, and the snails have opinions. For years they delivered every envelope to the town gate first, where a clerk read it a welcome speech. Nobody enjoyed the speech twice. This autumn the snails simply stopped going there. Now they slide straight to whichever window you last leaned out of, leaving a silver ribbon on the cobbles. Newcomers still hear the speech once, with cake. Everyone else gets their mail warm.
