The locksmith of Copper Lane keeps six keys that open no locks. At closing time she lays them on the counter and winds the gramophone. They rise on their little teeth and dance.

Tonight a customer stayed to watch. He had come for a spare key and forgotten which door it belonged to.

The smallest dancer rested in his palm when the music ended. He left without remembering, smiling as though he had found somewhere better to go.
