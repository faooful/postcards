On the river island of Quillbend, the ferrywoman keeps a basket of things nobody claimed. Tonight a single striped mitten sat at the top, with no tag and no owner. She didn't toss it overboard. She turned it inside out and found a stitched word, *Upper Hollow*, and rowed it upstream by lantern light. A shepherd boy was waiting on the bank, one hand pink with cold. He laughed so hard that the herons complained. The basket rode home lighter, and the river hummed along.
