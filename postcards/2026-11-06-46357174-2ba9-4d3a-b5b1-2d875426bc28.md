In the bazaar at Qasr Velo, the spices had always been shouted out in one long breathless sentence, and nobody could find the saffron. This week three old tortoises took over. They set out three painted trays at the front and laid little tiles along the counter, one per jar, each with a tiny brass hook so you could lift it away. By noon the merchants had stopped shouting. A child found cardamom on the first try and laughed so loudly the awning shook loose a shower of cinnamon dust.
