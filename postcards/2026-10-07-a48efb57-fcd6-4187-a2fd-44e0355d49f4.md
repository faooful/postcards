The fen cartographers had redrawn the marsh road four times, and each draft left a small honest gap where the water had moved overnight. This morning they walked it once more, quill cases rattling, and answered every marginal note pinned to the survey board. By noon the road sat true to the reeds, the footbridge legend matched its ink, and the last query in the margin was answered in a fair, unhurried hand. They rolled the map, warm from the sun, and agreed it was ready for the guild's seal.
