In the hill town of Pellwick, the guild of mapmakers had always worked at narrow desks, folding every chart into tidy strips. This autumn a stubborn apprentice pushed two desks together, then four, then dragged them into the square. By noon the whole valley lay open on the cobbles: rivers, goat paths, the little bakery by the ford. Pigeons strolled across the mountains. The old guildmaster grumbled, then took off his shoes and walked the coastline barefoot, *delighted* to see it all at once.
