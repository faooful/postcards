The mule train reached Halvern Gap a week late, and by then the survey camp had stopped quarreling about placement. The first lasting snowfall had settled every tent exactly where it stood, so when cooks argued and mules wandered, the campsite kept its shape. Marshal Odile walked the rows at dusk, delighted that her map still matched the world — the same crooked sequence of canvas, patient under new weather, holding its ground while everything around it moved.
