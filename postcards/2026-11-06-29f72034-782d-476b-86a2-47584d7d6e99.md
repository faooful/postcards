At dawn the glassblowers of the dune village found their furnaces had gone quiet overnight, the way a song stops when someone forgets the chorus. Nobody panicked. The eldest hummed the first bar, a child remembered the second, and soon every chimney was breathing amber again. By noon the market stalls glittered with little bottles, each holding a single warm breeze. Travellers uncorked them on the road home and laughed when the wind remembered their names.
