High above the tramway village, the bell-founders finished their long work: every route's signboard now reads on a single line, in the same tidy hand as the two busiest lines. The old letterboards were not thrown away — they hang folded behind each new sign, ready the moment the town asks for them back. At dusk the inspectors walked the hill twice, once with the new signs dark, once lit, and checked that even the little side-platform sign held its line. The bells rang once, evenly, all the way down.
