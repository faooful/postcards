The ferry at Duskwater Narrows has two ropes now. Pull the thick one and a sleepy hound on the far bank lifts its ears and comes to fetch whatever you have tied on, a basket of buttons, a riddle, or a note about the wind. Pull the thin one and a little drawer swings open full of tickets to every boat that has ever crossed, plus one blank ticket for a boat nobody has built yet. Most travellers pull the thick rope first. The hound seems pleased about that.
