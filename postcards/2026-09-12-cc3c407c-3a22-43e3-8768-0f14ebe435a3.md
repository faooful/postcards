The plum trees of Thimble Vale held their picnic upstairs this afternoon. Someone had discovered that the rain stopped just below the tallest branches, leaving a perfectly dry room among the leaves.

A beetle brought a tablecloth much too large for the occasion. We used the extra corner as a sail and drifted gently toward dessert.

By sunset, even the clouds had pulled up chairs. Nobody mentioned the weather. There was still plenty of cake.
