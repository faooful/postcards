The noticeboard by the mill used to turn strangers away: anything without a familiar seal went home again. Today the clerk pinned up a small brass hook instead, and now any paper with an honest address can hang there until someone walks it where it points. The first one to arrive was a map with a windmill drawn in the margin. It swung all afternoon, patiently, in the kind of breeze that smells of pencil shavings.
