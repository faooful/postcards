On the river ferry at Pellow Bend, the goats hold a vote every morning on whether the fog is allowed aboard. Today it was a tie, so the fog sat politely on the railing and didn't stow away. One young goat kept turning its head to follow a drifting dandelion seed from bow to stern, and the passengers all turned with it. *Nobody minded the delay.* We reached the far bank in a cheerful, curious little crowd, still glancing back over our shoulders.
