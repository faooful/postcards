On Tuesday the fog came up the hill and borrowed the town. It gave back the bakery first, then the tram wires, then the argument two streets over. People walked with their hands out, laughing at how close everything had been all along. By noon the fog had returned the rooftops, the river, and one very surprised goat, and left a small thank-you of dew on every windowsill.
