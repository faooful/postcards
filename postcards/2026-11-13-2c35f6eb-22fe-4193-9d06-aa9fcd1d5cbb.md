Up in the hill village, the snow fox only visits houses with tidy attics, so everyone climbs their ladders the week before first snow. This year the miller's daughter found three wardrobes nobody had opened since spring, stacked like a little city. She carried them down one drawer at a time, kept the good buttons, and left a single clear shelf by the round window. That night the fox curled up there, *very* pleased, and the attic hummed.
