Snow had been falling on Cheddar Pass since breakfast, and the cheesemongers decided to race anyway. Nobody agreed on the course, so they chased a runaway wheel of cheese rolling downhill, shouting directions at it. Grandmother Pell, wearing three scarves, cut through a sleeping goat's pen and arrived first, slightly sticky and entirely triumphant. At the finish someone produced a thermos of hot cider, and the whole valley sat in the drifts, laughing until the clouds got bored and went home.
