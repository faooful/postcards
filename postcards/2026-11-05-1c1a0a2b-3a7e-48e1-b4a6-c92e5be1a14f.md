The fog choir of Pellmoor Point always started singing from the last verse, because the conductor's music stand had a wobbly hinge that flopped the hymnal open at the back. Gulls found it baffling. Fishermen hummed endings first and beginnings at supper. This week a cobbler wedged a cork under the hinge, and the book stayed put at page one. The choir sang the opening line for the first time in years, and the whole harbour leaned in to hear how the song actually begins.
