The caravans left Brimble Junction at dawn, every last wagon, and the stationmaster's crow refused to wipe their names off the departures board. *Someone has to remember where they belong,* she croaked, and dusted the chalk until it shone. All afternoon the board held its little empty rows like open hands. By dusk a dust devil had danced through, two tortoises had asked directions, and a single lost wagon had rolled in, spotted its name, and parked beneath it, relieved.
